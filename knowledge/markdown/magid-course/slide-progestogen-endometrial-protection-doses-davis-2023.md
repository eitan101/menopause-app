# Progestogen Dosing for Endometrial Protection (Sequential vs. Continuous)

> **Layer 1 Source Reference:** [`WhatsApp Image 2026-11-09 at 21.20.49.jpeg`](../../sources/magid-course/WhatsApp%20Image%202026-11-09%20at%2021.20.49.jpeg)
> **Collection:** Magid Institute Practical Menopause Course (Dr. Renana Teomim MD Annotated Slide) | **Original Format:** `JPEG`
> **Primary Literature Citation on Slide:** S. R. Davis et al., *The 2023 Practitioner's Toolkit for Managing Menopause*, **Climacteric**, 2023; 26(6):517–536 (`DOI: 10.1080/13697137.2023.2258783`).

## Sequential Progestogen (`Sequential P`) — Daily Dose for 12–14 Days per Month for Endometrial Protection

| Israeli Trade Name Annotation | Progestogen Formulation | With Low Dose Estrogen (`Low dose E`) | With Mid to Highest Dose Estrogen (`Mid to highest dose E`) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Duphaston** | **Dydrogesterone (oral)** | `5 mg` | `10 mg` |
| **Utrogestan** | **Micronized progesterone (oral)** | `200 mg` *(efficacy of lower dose not established)* | `200 mg` |
| — | **Medroxyprogesterone acetate (oral)** | `5 mg` | `5–10 mg` |
| — | **Norethisterone acetate (oral)** | `1.25 mg – 2.5 mg` | `2.5–5 mg` |
| **Evorel conti** *(patch component)* | **Transdermal norethisterone acetate (with estradiol) patch** | — | releases `0.140–0.250 mg / day` |

## Continuous Progestogen (`Continuous P`) — Daily Dose for Endometrial Protection

| Israeli Trade Name Annotation | Progestogen Formulation | With Low Dose Estrogen (`Low dose E`) | With Mid to Highest Dose Estrogen (`Mid to highest dose E`) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Duphaston** | **Dydrogesterone (oral)** | `2.5–5 mg` | `5–10 mg` |
| *(Angeliq)* | **Drospirenone (oral)** | `2.0 mg` | — |
| **Utrogestan** | **Micronized progesterone (oral)^** | `100 mg` | `100 mg` for mid dose E *(however, this dose may not always provide sufficient endometrial protection with highest dose E)* |
| — | **Medroxyprogesterone acetate (oral)** | `2.5 mg` | `2.5–5 mg` |
| *(Eviana / Activelle)* | **Norethisterone acetate (oral)** | `0.1 mg` with `0.5 mg` estradiol<br>`0.5 mg` with `1.0 mg` estradiol | `1.0 mg – 2.5 mg` |
| **Evorel conti** | **Transdermal norethisterone acetate (with estradiol) patch** | — | releases `0.140–0.250 mg / day` |
| — | **Levonorgestrel (with estradiol) patch** | — | releases `0.015 mg / day` |
| **Mirena** | **LNG-IUD** | Device initially releasing `20 mcg/day` *(suitable across estrogen doses)* | Device initially releasing `20 mcg/day` *(suitable across estrogen doses)* |
