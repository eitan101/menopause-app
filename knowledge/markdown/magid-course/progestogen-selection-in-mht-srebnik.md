## Slide 2: Historical Timeline of MHT & Progestogens — ציר הזמן ההיסטורי ("רכבת ההרים" של MHT)

גרף מגמות השימוש ב-HRT בין השנים `1940–2010+` (בליווי תמונת רכבת הרים):

- **`1942`** — טיפול הורמונלי באסטרוגן בלבד להקלת הסימפטומים *(עלייה חדה בשימוש)*
- **`1960`** — עליה בשכיחות סרטן הרחם *(ירידה בשימוש)*
- **`1970`** — שילוב פרוגסטרון למניעת סרטן הרחם *(חידוש העלייה בשימוש)*
- **`1985`** — מחקר האחיות - `HRT` מפחית הסיכון למחלות לב *(זינוק לשיא השימוש סביב שנת 2000)*
- **`2002 – WHI`** — צניחה חדה בשימוש בעקבות פרסום ממצאי ה-WHI הראשוניים, והתאוששות מתונה לאחר `2010`.

## Slide 3: Role & Classification of Progestogens in MHT — תפקיד וסיווג הפרוגסטוגנים

### Progestines in MHT
- **Estrogen**- to relieve symptoms
- **Progestins**- for endometrial protection (to prevent oestrogen-induced endometrial hyperplasia or endometrial cancer)

| `Natural / neutral progestogens` | `Synthetic progestogens derived from` |
| :--- | :--- |
| • **progesterone**<br>• **Dydrogesterone** | • **21-carbon steroid precursors** *(נגזרות פרגנאן / נורפרגנאן, כגון MPA)*<br>• **19-carbon steroid precursors** *(נגזרות נור-טסטוסטרון, כגון NETA, LNG)*<br>• **spironolactone** *(דרוספירנון)* |

## Slide 4: Off-Target Steroid Receptor Binding — קישור לרצפטורים סטרואידליים נוספים

- **synthetic progestogens, also known as progestins**, might bind to other steroid receptors, including **glucocorticoid**, **mineralocorticoid**, **androgen** and **oestrogen** receptors, thereby inducing additional effects, particularly relating to **breast cancer** and **CVD risk**.

## Slide 10: E3N Cohort Study Design (Fournier 2008)

*Unequal risks for breast cancer associated with different hormone replacement therapies: results from the E3N cohort study — Fournier 2008*
- **The E3N cohort**
- **`80,377` women aged `40-65`**. Analysis was limited to postmenopausal women.
- Biannual self-administered questionnaires completed from `1990` to `2002`. Mean duration **`8.1` postmenopausal years**.

## Slide 12: Collaborative Group Meta-Analysis — Lancet 2019 (Design & Limitations)

*Type and timing of menopausal hormone therapy and breast cancer risk: individual participant meta-analysis of the worldwide epidemiological evidence — Collaborative Group on Hormonal Factors in Breast Cancer, Lancet. 2019 Sep 28; 394(10204): 1159–1168.*
- Epidemiological study
- Meta-analysis of **`58` studies**: `143,887` postmenopausal women with invasive breast cancer (cases) and `424,972` without breast cancer (controls)
- Prospective data: `108,647` cases, `55,575 (51%)` had used MHT.
- **Problems: observational only, no RCTs** *(מודגש באדום)*

## Slide 14: WHI Long-Term RCT Follow-Up (Chlebowski et al., JAMA 2020)

*WHI breast cancer incidence — Chlebowski et al., JAMA 2020. doi: 10.1001/jama.2020.9482.*
- Long-term follow-up of **2 RCTs**: `27,347` postmenopausal women aged `50 - 79 years`
- No prior breast cancer; enrolled `1993 - 1998`, follow-up through **December 31, 2017** (~20+ years).
- **`16,608` women with a uterus (`CEE + MPA` vs placebo till 2002):** בגרף (`Favors placebo`), עלייה מובהקת ומתמשכת בהיארעות סרטן שד פולשני (`p < .001`), ללא עלייה מובהקת בתמותה מסרטן שד לאורך המעקב.
- **`10,739` women with prior hysterectomy (`CEE alone` vs placebo till 2004):** בגרף (`Favors HT`), **ירידה מובהקת בהיארעות סרטן שד פולשני וירידה מובהקת בתמותה מסרטן שד** לאורך 20+ שנות מעקב.

## Slide 15: Vinogradova et al., BMJ 2020 — Study Design

*Use of hormone replacement therapy and risk of breast cancer: nested case-control studies using the QResearch and CPRD databases — Vinogradova Y, Coupland C, Hippisley-Cox J. BMJ. 2020 (Oct 28, 2020). doi: 10.1136/bmj.m3873.*
- ✓ Two nested case-control studies (UK primary care databases `QResearch` and `CPRD`, 21 years: `1998–2018`), matched by age, general practice, and index date.
- ✓ Women `50-79y`: **`98,611` diagnosed with breast cancer** compared to **`457,498` controls** (total of **`556,109` women**).

## Slide 16: Vinogradova et al., BMJ 2020 — Exposure & Cohort Characteristics

- Used HRT prior to one year before the index date:
  - `33,703 (34%)` women with breast cancer
  - `134,391 (31%)` controls
- A large proportion of women switched between different combinations of oestrogens and progestogens, so each hormonal preparation was analysed as a separate exposure.
- Cases (CA) were more likely to be **overweight or obese** (`53% v 50%`), **former smokers** (`29% v 27%`), have a record of **benign breast disease** (`9% v 6%`) or other cancers (`3.1% v 2.6%`), or have a **family history** of breast cancer (`4% v 2.5%`).

## Slide 20: Danish Osteoporosis Prevention Study (DOPS) RCT — Design (BMJ 2012)

*Effect of hormone replacement therapy on cardiovascular events in recently postmenopausal women: randomized trial — BMJ 2012;345:e6409 (Published 9 October 2012).*
- **Objective:** To investigate the long term effect of HRT on cardiovascular outcomes in recently postmenopausal women.
- **Design:** Denmark, `1990-93`. Open label, randomised controlled trial.
- **Population:** **`1006` healthy women aged `45-58` (average `50`)** who were recently postmenopausal (for `7 months`):
  - `502` women – **HRT** (`estradiol & norethisterone acetate` ברחם קיים, או אסטרדיול בלבד לאחר כריתת רחם)
  - `504` women – **no treatment (control)**
- **Duration:** **Intervention - `11 years` | Follow up - `16 years`**.

## Slide 28: REPLENISH Trial & Sequential Progestogen Duration Rule

- **The REPLENISH trial** demonstrated **`0%` endometrial hyperplasia at 12 months** with combined estradiol/progesterone.
- **Sequential use of progestogens protects the endometrium if exposure lasts for at least `12 days/month`; longer intervals are not safe.**

## Slide 29: Micronized Progesterone Dosing & Routes (Stute et al. 2016)

### Dosing and regimen (*Stute P, Neulen J, Wildt L. Climacteric. 2016*)
- **Oral micronized progesterone** provides endometrial protection when given:
  - **Sequentially at `200 mg/day` for `12–14 days` per month**, for up to `5 years`, or
  - **Continuously at lower daily doses** (`100 mg/day`) to avoid withdrawal bleeding.
- **Transdermal micronized progesterone does NOT reliably provide endometrial protection and should not be relied upon for this purpose.**
- *Vaginal micronized progesterone* (לפי סקירת 2016): may provide endometrial protection if applied sequentially for at least `10 days/month` at `4% (45 mg/day)` or every other day at `100 mg/day` *(ראו עדכון מחמיר בסקירה השיטתית מ-2024 בשקופית 32 להלן!)*.

## Slide 34: E2 + NETA Effect on Bone Mineral Content — השפעת NETA על תכולת מינרלים ובניית עצם

### `E2+NETA in bone mineral content`
- **מחקר שבדק את השפעת `norethisterone` על אובדן עצם בנשים בגיל המעבר לעומת פלסבו** (*Obstetrics and Gynecology, 01 Dec 1985, 66(6):789-792*):
