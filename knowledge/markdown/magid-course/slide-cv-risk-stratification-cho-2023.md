## Traffic-Light CV Risk Stratification for MHT

| Risk Tier | Traffic Light | Patient Profile & Clinical Risk Factors |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Low Risk HT** | 🟢 Green | • Recent menopause<br>• Normal weight<br>• Normal blood pressure<br>• Physically active<br>• **10-year ASCVD risk < 5%**<br>• Low risk for breast cancer |
| **Intermediate Risk HT** | 🟡 Yellow | • Diabetes<br>• Smoking<br>• Hypertension (`HTN`)<br>• Obesity<br>• Sedentary / Limited mobility<br>• Autoimmune disease<br>• Hyperlipidemia<br>• Metabolic syndrome<br>• **10-year ASCVD risk ≥ 5–10%**<br>• High risk for breast cancer |
| **High Risk HT\*** | 🔴 Red | • Congenital heart disease<br>• `ASCVD` / `CAD` / `PAD`<br>• Venous thrombosis or pulmonary embolism (`VTE` / `PE`)<br>• Stroke / `TIA` or myocardial infarction (`MI`)<br>• Breast cancer<br>• **10-year ASCVD risk ≥ 10%\*** |
