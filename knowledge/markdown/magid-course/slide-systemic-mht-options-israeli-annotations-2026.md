### Footnote Highlighted on Slide
> **\*** *Data support the off-label use of a 52-mg levonorgestrel-releasing intrauterine device combined with estrogen to provide endometrial protection for up to 5 years* (Voedisch AJ. *Use of progestin-containing intrauterine systems in hormone therapy regimens: what are the data?* **Menopause** 2026;33:86–7. `doi: 10.1097/GME.0000000000002672`).
