# הערכת סיכון CV — 4-Step Cardiovascular Evaluation for Systemic HT

> **Layer 1 Source Reference:** [`WhatsApp Image 2026-11-09 at 19.36.4.jpeg`](../../sources/magid-course/WhatsApp%20Image%202026-11-09%20at%2019.36.4.jpeg)
> **Collection:** Magid Institute Practical Menopause Course (Dr. Renana Teomim MD Lecture Slide) | **Original Format:** `JPEG`
> **Primary Literature Citation on Slide:** O'Kelly AC et al., *Pregnancy and Reproductive Risk Factors for Cardiovascular Disease in Women.* **Circ Res.** 2022 Feb.

## Step-by-Step CV Assessment Algorithm

### Step 1: Evaluate Age, Time Since Menopause, and Symptoms
* **Optimal Candidate Window:** `< 10 years` from final menstrual cycle, `< 60 years old`, and **bothersome vasomotor symptoms**.

### Step 2: Perform ASCVD Risk Assessment and Exclude HT Contraindications

| ASCVD Risk Factors (Including Sex-Specific Enhancers) | Contraindications to Systemic HT |
| :--- | :--- |
| • Hyperlipidemia<br>• Hypertension<br>• Diabetes<br>• Family history of premature CVD in first-degree relative (`men < 55` or `women < 65` years of age)<br>• Obesity (`BMI > 30 kg/m²`)<br>• Physical inactivity<br>• Cigarette smoking<br>• Coronary calcification (moderate risk: `CAC 1–99`; high risk: `CAC > 100`)<br>• **History of preeclampsia**<br>• **History of systemic autoimmune collagen-vascular disease** (e.g., lupus, rheumatoid arthritis) | • Coronary heart disease, stroke, `TIA`<br>• Breast or endometrial cancer<br>• History of pulmonary embolus, venous thrombosis, or clotting disorder<br>• Active liver disease<br>• Undiagnosed abnormal vaginal bleeding |

### Step 3: Evaluate Risk Category and Select Route

| Tier | Clinical Recommendation | Criteria |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 🟢 **Green** | **May consider HT** (oral or transdermal) | `ASCVD risk < 5%` (low risk) **and** `≤ 1` CVD risk factor |
| 🟡 **Yellow** | **May consider HT, transdermal formulation** | `ASCVD risk 5–10%` **OR** `ASCVD risk < 5%` with `≥ 2` CVD risk factors |
| 🔴 **Red** | **Not recommended to use HT** | `Age ≥ 60` **OR** `> 10 years` since menopause onset **OR** `ASCVD risk > 10%` |

### Step 4: Longitudinal Monitoring
* **Ensure routine follow-up with periodic re-evaluation of risks and benefits.**
