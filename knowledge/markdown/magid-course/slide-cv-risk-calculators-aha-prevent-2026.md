## 10-Year CVD Risk Decision Rules (AHA PREVENT Equation)

| 10-Year CVD Risk Tier | Traffic Light | MHT Prescribing Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **LOW (`< 5%`)** *(Low `< 3%`, Borderline `3 to < 5%`)* | 🟢 Green | **➔ MHT appropriate** (systemic HT is appropriate for symptomatic women with low or borderline cardiovascular risk) |
| **INTERMEDIATE (`5–10%`)** *(`5 to < 10%` or cardiometabolic risk factors)* | 🟡 Yellow | **➔ Transdermal (`T.D`) estrogen preferred** |
| **HIGH RISK (`≥ 10%`)** | 🔴 Red | **➔ DO NOT use systemic HT** |

## Verbatim Clinical Guidance Highlighted on Slide

> "The American Heart Association **PREVENT** equation provides sex-specific categorizes of 10-year risk as **low** (less than 3%), **borderline** (3–less than 5%), **intermediate** (5–less than 10%), and **high** (10% or more). In general, **systemic HT is appropriate for symptomatic women with low or borderline cardiovascular risk, whereas transdermal estrogen is preferred for women with intermediate risk or cardiometabolic risk factors.**⁶³ **Systemic HT should generally be avoided in women with high predicted cardiovascular risk (10-year PREVENT risk of 10% or more)**; established atherosclerotic CVD such as prior myocardial infarction, stroke or transient ischemic attack, or venous thromboembolism; or estrogen-sensitive cancers.⁶⁰"
