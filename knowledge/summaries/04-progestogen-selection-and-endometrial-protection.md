## 1. Molecular Receptor Profiles & Clinical Trade-Offs Across Progestogens

Unopposed systemic estrogen in women with an intact uterus causes a dose- and duration-dependent surge in endometrial hyperplasia and adenocarcinoma; adding an adequate dose and duration of progestogen (or `bazedoxifene`) is mandatory ([`05-hrt-regimens-and-progestogen-doses.md`](../markdown/red-whale/05-hrt-regimens-and-progestogen-doses.md#unopposed-oestrogen-increases-endometrial-cancer-risk)). However, progestogens differ substantially in steroid-receptor cross-reactivity (`PR`, `AR` androgen, `MR` mineralocorticoid, `GR` glucocorticoid, `GABA-A` neurosteroid metabolites) and clinical safety ([`progestogen-selection-in-mht-srebnik.md` §Slide 38–39](../markdown/magid-course/progestogen-selection-in-mht-srebnik.md#slide-39-practical-clinical-comparison-of-progestogens-in-israel--טבלת-השוואה-קלינית-מסכמת); [`menopause-mht-treatment-renana-teomim.md` §Slide 6](../markdown/magid-course/menopause-mht-treatment-renana-teomim.md#slide-6-spectrum-of-hormonal-activities-of-progestogens--פעילות-הורמונלית-של-פרוגסטוגנים)):

| Progestogen / Option (Israeli Brands) | Receptor & Metabolic Profile | Breast & VTE Safety | Endometrial / Bleeding Control & Bone | Clinical Pearls & Cautions |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Micronized Progesterone (`MP`)**<br>*`Utrogestan` (`100` / `200 mg`), `Gepretix`* | Bioidentical; weak anti-mineralocorticoid; no androgenic/glucocorticoid activity; `allopregnanolone` `GABA-A` sedative metabolite | **Safest:** No excess BC risk `<5y` (`E3N`, `Vinogradova 2020`); **neutral VTE** (`HR ~0.9–1.0` with TD E2) | Good in RCTs (`REPLENISH`), slightly higher erratic spotting & requires strict adherence / dose-scaling at high E2; bone neutral | Take **at bedtime** (improves sleep latency/depth; avoids daytime drowsiness). **Contraindicated in peanut/soy allergy** (soy lecithin capsule excipient) |
| **Dydrogesterone**<br>*`Duphaston 10 mg`, `Femoston 1/10, 2/10`, `Femoston Conti 1/5, 0.5/2.5`* | Retroprogesterone stereoisomer; highly selective `PR` agonist; **no** androgenic, estrogenic, or glucocorticoid effects; non-sedating | **Very high safety:** No excess BC `<5y` and lowest excess `>5y` (`OR 1.15–1.24`); **neutral VTE** | Strong endometrial protection & better cycle/bleeding predictability than MP; bone neutral | Ideal oral alternative when `Utrogestan` causes drowsiness, GI intolerance, or erratic bleeding |
| **`52 mg LNG-IUS`**<br>*`Mirena`* | C19 19-nortestosterone derivative; potent local endometrial atrophy (`20 mcg/d` initial release), low systemic levels | **VTE neutral**; **Breast:** observational studies show small BC risk increase **similar to systemic synthetic progestins** (`Vinogradova` / Red Whale #7) | **Gold standard** for heavy perimenopausal bleeding, high endometrial risk (`BMI ≥40`), and **`GLP-1` users**; covers **all** estrogen doses! | Valid for **5 years** as MHT endometrial protection (even if inserted `≥45y`!). Check strings/position if new bleeding occurs |
| **Norethisterone Acetate (`NETA`)**<br>*`Activelle`, `Eviana`, `Novofem`, `Trisequens`, `Evorel Conti` patch* | C19 testosterone derivative; partial **androgenic** activity | Higher BC risk on long-term use (`Vinogradova OR ~1.88 >5y`, though neutral in small `DOPS` RCT); transdermal `Evorel Conti` avoids hepatic first-pass VTE | **Excellent bleeding suppression** ("dry" endometrium) & **unique independent anabolic bone-building boost** (`Srebnik Slides 34–36`) | Can cause acne, seborrhea, hirsutism, or PMS-like irritability/mood swings in androgen-sensitive women |
| **Drospirenone (`DRSP`)**<br>*`Angeliq` (`1mg E2 + 2mg DRSP`)* | Spironolactone analogue; **anti-androgenic** + **anti-mineralocorticoid** (aldosterone antagonist) | Intermediate synthetic progestin BC profile; oral combination carries standard oral VTE caution | Good amenorrhea rate; reduces bloating/fluid retention and **mildly lowers blood pressure** | **Monitor potassium / caution** with renal impairment (e.g. solitary kidney/CKD) or `ACEi` / `ARBs` / `NSAIDs` / K⁺-sparing diuretics |
| **Medroxyprogesterone Acetate (`MPA`)** | C21 17-OH progesterone derivative; partial **glucocorticoid** + weak androgenic activity | Highest VTE (`OR ~1.7–2.2`) & WHI breast cancer (`HR 1.28`) signal | Reliable endometrial suppression | Rarely first-line in modern MHT when MP, dydrogesterone, `Mirena`, or `NETA`/`DRSP` are available |
| **TSEC (`CEE 0.45mg + Bazedoxifene 20mg`)**<br>*`Duavive`* | Progestogen-free! `SERM` antagonizes estrogen at **endometrium and breast** | Favorable breast density/mastalgia profile; no BC signal in 2-yr `SMART` trials; oral CEE VTE caution | Excellent amenorrhea rate (`>83%`); preserves bone density | Go-to option for postmenopausal women with intact uterus and **severe progestogen intolerance** or severe mastalgia |

## 2. BMS / IMS Proportional Progestogen Dosing by Estrogen Dose Tier

A critical modern safety rule (`BMS 2024` / `Red Whale #5` / `Davis 2023 Toolkit`): **when systemic estradiol is escalated to moderate or high doses, oral progestogen must be escalated proportionally** (whereas `52 mg Mirena` protects across **all** estrogen doses for 5 years) ([`05-hrt-regimens-and-progestogen-doses.md` §Progestogen doses](../markdown/red-whale/05-hrt-regimens-and-progestogen-doses.md#hrt-dose-matching-progestogen-doses); [`slide-progestogen-endometrial-protection-doses-davis-2023.md`](../markdown/magid-course/slide-progestogen-endometrial-protection-doses-davis-2023.md); [`hebrew-summary-hrt-and-progesterone.md` §Progestogen Dosing](../markdown/red-whale/hebrew-summary-hrt-and-progesterone.md#progestogen-dosing--מינוני-פרוגסטרון)):

| Progestogen | Regimen Type | Ultra-Low to Standard E2 (`≤50 mcg` patch / `≤1.5 mg` gel / `≤2 mg` oral) | Moderate E2 (`75 mcg` patch / `2.25 mg` gel / `3 mg` oral) | High E2 (`100 mcg` patch / `3 mg` gel / `4 mg` oral) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Micronized Progesterone (`Utrogestan`)** | **Continuous** (daily) | **`100 mg`** nightly | `100 mg` *(consider `200 mg` if breakthrough bleeding or risk factors)* | **`200 mg`** nightly |
| **Micronized Progesterone (`Utrogestan`)** | **Sequential** (`12–14 d/mo`) | **`200 mg`** nightly × `12–14 d` | `200 mg` *(consider `300 mg`)* × `12–14 d` | **`300 mg`** nightly × `12 d` |
| **Dydrogesterone (`Duphaston`)** | **Continuous** / **Sequential** | Continuous: **`5 mg/d`** (`2.5 mg` with `0.5mg` E2)<br>Sequential: **`10 mg/d`** × `14 d` | Continuous: `5–10 mg/d`<br>Sequential: `10 mg/d` × `14 d` | Continuous: **`10 mg/d`** *(up to `20 mg` specialist)*<br>Sequential: **`10–20 mg/d`** × `14 d` |
| **Medroxyprogesterone (`MPA`)** | **Continuous** / **Sequential** | Continuous: `2.5–5 mg/d`<br>Sequential: `5–10 mg/d` × `12–14 d` | Continuous: `5 mg/d`<br>Sequential: `10 mg/d` × `12–14 d` | Continuous: `5–10 mg/d`<br>Sequential: `10–20 mg/d` × `12–14 d` |
| **Norethisterone (`NETA`)** | **Continuous** / **Sequential** | Continuous: `0.5–1 mg/d` (`0.1 mg` in `Eviana`; `170 mcg/d` in `Evorel Conti`)<br>Sequential: `1–2.5 mg/d` × `12–14 d` | Continuous: `1–2.5 mg/d`<br>Sequential: `2.5–5 mg/d` × `12–14 d` | Continuous: `2.5–5 mg/d`<br>Sequential: `5 mg/d` × `12–14 d` |
| **`52 mg LNG-IUS` (`Mirena`)** | **Continuous Intrauterine** | **1 device for up to 5 years** | **1 device for up to 5 years** | **1 device for up to 5 years** |

## 3. Crucial Off-Label & Contraceptive Pitfalls

* **Progestogen-Only Contraceptive Pills (`POPs`) Are NOT Standard HRT Endometrial Protection:** Standard contraceptive doses of `norethisterone 350 mcg` (`Noriday`), `desogestrel 75 mcg` (`Cerazette`/`Diamilla`), `drospirenone 4 mg` (`Slynd`), or subdermal `etonogestrel` implant (`Nexplanon`) **are NOT licensed or proven for MHT endometrial protection**! Women taking a POP for contraception who start HRT must either switch to **`52mg Mirena` + estrogen** or take a **combined sequential HRT regimen alongside their POP** *(BMS notes a specialist last-resort off-label option of double-dose `desogestrel 150 mcg/d` only in severe intolerance after full counseling)* ([`06-hrt-availability-switching-off-licence.md` §Off-licence progestogen](../markdown/red-whale/06-hrt-availability-switching-off-licence.md#off-licence-progestogen-prescribing); [`acog-cpg-13-vasomotor-sleep-mood-2026.md` §Hormone Therapy](../markdown/magid-course/acog-cpg-13-vasomotor-sleep-mood-2026.md#hormone-therapy)).
* **Lower-Dose Hormonal IUDs (`Kyleena 19.5 mg`, `Jaydess/Skyla 13.5 mg`):** **Do NOT provide MHT endometrial protection** — only the **`52 mg` LNG-IUS (`Mirena` / `Levosert`)** is endorsed, and it **must be replaced at 5 years** when used for MHT ([`05-hrt-regimens-and-progestogen-doses.md`](../markdown/red-whale/05-hrt-regimens-and-progestogen-doses.md#licensed-progestogen-regimens); [`practical-menopause-course-session5-cases.md` §Case 9](../markdown/magid-course/practical-menopause-course-session5-cases.md#case-9-perimenopausal-53-year-old-kidney-donor-with-expired-mirena-8-years-irregular-bleeding-severe-pms--vms--מקרה-9-page-9)).
* **Vaginal Micronized Progesterone (`Utrogestan PV`):** Off-label option sometimes used in severe oral progestogen systemic intolerance (bypasses hepatic/CNS neurosteroid sedation via uterine first-pass effect at identical mg dose to oral), **but long-term (`>1–3y`) endometrial safety RCT evidence is limited (`ELITE` / `Stute 2016/2024`)** — counsel patients explicitly and monitor *any* unscheduled bleeding promptly, or prefer `Mirena` / `Duavive` / `Duphaston` first ([`05-hrt-regimens-and-progestogen-doses.md` §Progestogen intolerance](../markdown/red-whale/05-hrt-regimens-and-progestogen-doses.md#vaginal-administration-of-micronised-progesterone); [`progestogen-selection-in-mht-srebnik.md` §Slide 29](../markdown/magid-course/progestogen-selection-in-mht-srebnik.md#slide-29-micronized-progesterone-dosing--routes-stute-et-al-2016)).
