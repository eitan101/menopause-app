## 1. Genitourinary Syndrome of Menopause (`GSM`) & Local Vaginal Therapies

Unlike vasomotor symptoms (which often attenuate over years), **`GSM` is chronic and progressive** without local treatment, and ~10%–25% of women taking *systemic* MHT still require concurrent local vaginal therapy ([`02-nice-guideline-menopause-2024.md` §GSM](../markdown/red-whale/02-nice-guideline-menopause-2024.md#management-of-genitourinary-symptoms-of-menopause)).

* **First-Line Local Vaginal Estrogen (Can Be Used Alone OR Alongside Systemic MHT, Indefinitely):**
  * **`Vagifem 10 mcg`** (estradiol vaginal tablet): nightly for **2 weeks**, then **twice weekly** maintenance.
  * **`Ovestin`** (estriol `0.1%` cream or `0.5 mg` ovules): nightly for **2 weeks**, then **twice weekly** maintenance.
  * **Safety Pearls (`NICE NG23 2024`):** Minimal systemic absorption -> **NO progestogen required** for endometrial protection, **no** VTE/CVD/breast cancer risk increase in the general population, and symptoms recur if stopped (long-term maintenance is safe and expected) ([`02-nice-guideline-menopause-2024.md` §NICE GSM recommendations](../markdown/red-whale/02-nice-guideline-menopause-2024.md#what-does-nice-recommend-for-the-management-of-genitourinary-symptoms)).
* **Alternative Approved GSM Therapies:**
  * **Non-hormonal vaginal moisturizers** (2–3×/week regularly) + **water/silicone lubricants** (during intercourse) — always offer alongside or as first-line when hormones are avoided.
  * **`Intrarosa` (`Prasterone` / `DHEA 6.5 mg` nightly vaginal pessary):** Converted intracrinologically inside vaginal mucosal cells to local estrogens and androgens; improves dyspareunia, dryness, and sexual sensation.
  * **`Ospemifene 60 mg/d` oral (`SERM`):** Oral estrogen agonist on vaginal epithelium for women unable to use vaginal preparations.
  * **Vaginal Laser (`CO₂` / Erbium):** `NICE 2024` advises **against routine use** outside clinical trials due to lack of sham-controlled superiority and potential tissue injury ([`02-nice-guideline-menopause-2024.md` §GSM](../markdown/red-whale/02-nice-guideline-menopause-2024.md#what-does-nice-recommend-for-the-management-of-genitourinary-symptoms)).
* **GSM in Women with Prior Breast Cancer (`NICE 2024`):**
  * Start with **non-hormonal moisturizers and lubricants**.
  * If refractory and distressing, **low-dose vaginal estrogen** may be considered in shared decision-making with the oncology team:
    * **ER-negative breast cancer:** Unlikely to increase recurrence.
    * **Taking `Tamoxifen`:** Competitive ER blockade at breast tissue buffers any trace systemic estradiol absorption -> vaginal estrogen is generally acceptable.
    * **Taking `Aromatase Inhibitors` (`letrozole`, `anastrozole`, `exemestane`):** Because AIs rely on near-zero circulating estradiol, even tiny transient absorption spikes warrant **oncologist discussion** (or consideration of switching AI to tamoxifen if appropriate) ([`02-nice-guideline-menopause-2024.md` §Women with breast cancer](../markdown/red-whale/02-nice-guideline-menopause-2024.md#women-with-breast-cancer)).

## 2. Transdermal Testosterone for Hypoactive Sexual Desire Disorder (`HSDD`)

* **Clinical Indication (`NICE` / `BMS` / `IMS Global Consensus` / `ASRM POI 2024`):** Consider a **`3–6 month` trial** of physiological female-dose transdermal testosterone **only for persistent, distressing low sexual desire (`HSDD`)** after: **(1)** biopsychosocial/relationship assessment, **(2)** treating `GSM`/dyspareunia, and **(3)** optimizing systemic estrogen (preferring **transdermal E2** over oral E2 so `SHBG` is not elevated!) — *testosterone is NOT effective for brain fog, fatigue, depression, VMS, or bone protection alone* ([`mht-in-brca-carriers-srebnik.md` §Slides 38–44](../markdown/magid-course/mht-in-brca-carriers-srebnik.md#slide-43-transdermal-testosterone-dosing--short-term-safety-profile--מינון-ופרופיל-בטיחות-של-טסטוסטרון-טרנסדרמלי); [`asrm-eshre-ims-poi-guideline-2024.md` §Section 4](../markdown/magid-course/asrm-eshre-ims-poi-guideline-2024.md#section-4-poi-treatment--hormone-therapy-ht-testosterone-non-hormonal-complementary-lifestyle--puberty-induction-figure-3)).
* **Practical Dosing & Monitoring:**
  * **`Testogel 1%` (`50 mg/5g` male sachet — off-label female titration):** ~**`5 mg/day`** (`1/10th` to `1/8th` of a sachet daily, so 1 sachet lasts **8–10 days**), applied to clean dry lower abdomen/outer thigh (`AndroFeme 1%` female cream `0.5 mL = 5 mg/d` where available).
  * **Laboratory Monitoring:** Check **baseline Total Testosterone (`TT`) + `SHBG`** (to confirm levels are not already in the upper female range), repeat at **`3–6 weeks` / `3 months`**, and **every `6–12 months`** to keep `TT` strictly within the **physiological premenopausal female range** (avoiding acne, hirsutism, alopecia, or irreversible voice deepening/clitoromegaly). Stop at `6 months` if no clinical benefit ([`mht-in-brca-carriers-srebnik.md` §Slide 43](../markdown/magid-course/mht-in-brca-carriers-srebnik.md#slide-43-transdermal-testosterone-dosing--short-term-safety-profile--מינון-ופרופיל-בטיחות-של-טסטוסטרון-טרנסדרמלי)).

## 3. Evidence-Based Non-Hormonal Pharmacologic Treatments for VMS & Sleep

Synthesized from **ACOG CPG No. 13 (Nov 2026)** ([`acog-cpg-13-vasomotor-sleep-mood-2026.md` §Table 2](../markdown/magid-course/acog-cpg-13-vasomotor-sleep-mood-2026.md#table-2-evidence-based-nonhormonal-pharmacologic-treatment-options-for-menopausal-symptoms)) and **BMS Consensus Statement (July 2026)** ([`bms-consensus-non-hormonal-treatments-2026.md`](../markdown/magid-course/bms-consensus-non-hormonal-treatments-2026.md)):

| Drug Class & Agent | Effective VMS Dosage | Efficacy & Target Symptoms | Mandatory Safety, LFT & Drug-Interaction Rules |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **NK3 Receptor Antagonist**<br>**`Fezolinetant` (`Veoza`)** | **`45 mg` once daily** | *Strong / High (ACOG; NICE-approved 2026):* Rapid reduction in moderate-to-severe **VMS** (as early as day 1–7) + modest sleep improvement (`SKYLIGHT 1/2/4`, `DAYLIGHT`) | **Hepatotoxicity Monitoring:** Check baseline **`ALT`, `AST`, `ALP`, total & direct bilirubin** before starting (do not start if `ALT/AST ≥2× ULN`), then repeat at **months `1`, `2`, `3`** (`BMS` + `FDA`) **and months `6` & `9`** (`FDA/ACOG` boxed warning). Contraindicated in cirrhosis, severe renal impairment, or with **CYP1A2 inhibitors** (`fluvoxamine`, `ciprofloxacin`). *Note:* Dedicated breast cancer trials were still pending in UK license |
| **Dual NK1/NK3 Antagonist**<br>**`Elinzanetant` (`Lynkuet`)** | **`120 mg` once daily** | *Strong / High (ACOG; MHRA-licensed incl. HR+ breast cancer endocrine therapy `OASIS-4`!)*: Robust reduction in **both VMS and sleep disturbance** (`OASIS 1–4`) | **Simpler Liver Monitoring:** Check `LFTs` at **baseline** (`BMS` baseline only; `FDA/ACOG` baseline + **month `3`**). Contraindicated in pregnancy; avoid strong **CYP3A4 inhibitors** (reduce to `60 mg` with moderate CYP3A4 inhibitors) |
| **SNRIs**<br>**`Venlafaxine`** / **`Desvenlafaxine`** | `37.5–75 mg/d` (up to `150 mg/d`) / `50–100 mg/d` | *Strong / Moderate:* **48%–67% VMS reduction** within 1–2 weeks + treats anxiety/depression | **SAFE with `Tamoxifen`** (minimal `CYP2D6` inhibition)! Start at `37.5 mg` to minimize initial nausea; taper when stopping |
| **SSRIs**<br>**`Escitalopram`** / **`Citalopram`** / **`Paroxetine`** / **`Fluoxetine`** | Escitalopram `10–20 mg/d`; Citalopram `10–20 mg/d`; Paroxetine `7.5 mg/d` (FDA VMS) or `10–20 mg/d` | *Strong / Moderate:* **Escitalopram** ranked highest among SSRIs in network comparisons; treats concurrent mood/anxiety | 🛑 **CRITICAL `CYP2D6` CONTRAINDICATION:** **NEVER co-prescribe `Paroxetine` or `Fluoxetine` with `Tamoxifen`** — potent `CYP2D6` inhibition blocks conversion of tamoxifen to active `endoxifen`! Use `Venlafaxine`, `Escitalopram`, or `Citalopram` instead |
| **Gabapentinoids**<br>**`Gabapentin`** *(or `Pregabalin`)* | **`300–900 mg/d`** *(start `100–300 mg` at bedtime; titrate as needed)* | *Conditional (ACOG) / Option (BMS):* ~45%–60% VMS reduction + helpful when **nocturnal sweats/insomnia** predominate | Causes dizziness/somnolence (dose at bedtime); UK Schedule 3 controlled drug (misuse/dependence awareness) |
| **Anticholinergic**<br>**`Oxybutynin`** | `2.5–5 mg` bd IR or `5–15 mg` od XL *(off-label)* | ~70%–86% VMS reduction in RCTs + helps overactive bladder | Dry mouth (~50%), urinary retention; **avoid long-term use in older adults (`>65y`) due to cognitive decline / dementia association** (`ACOG` & `BMS`) |
| **α₂-Agonist**<br>**`Clonidine`** | `25–75 mcg` bd | Modest efficacy (~35%–40%); **ACOG 2026 suggests AGAINST as primary option** | Dry mouth, postural hypotension, insomnia, rebound hypertension on abrupt withdrawal |

## 4. Non-Pharmacologic Therapies & Herbal Supplements

* **Recommended Behavioral & Mind-Body Interventions:**
  * **Menopause-Specific `CBT` (`MENOS 1` & `MENOS 2`):** Endorsed by **NICE 2024**, **BMS 2026**, and **ACOG 2026** for **VMS bother/distress, menopause-related sleep disturbance, and depressive symptoms** (in-person, group, or digital self-help) ([`02-nice-guideline-menopause-2024.md` §CBT](../markdown/red-whale/02-nice-guideline-menopause-2024.md#cbt-for-vasomotor-symptoms-depressive-symptoms-and-insomnia); [`acog-cpg-13-vasomotor-sleep-mood-2026.md` §Nonpharmacologic](../markdown/magid-course/acog-cpg-13-vasomotor-sleep-mood-2026.md#nonpharmacologic-interventions)).
  * **Mindfulness-Based Interventions (`MBIs`) & Physical Exercise:** Improve **sleep quality, depression, and anxiety** (though exercise does not directly reduce VMS frequency) ([`acog-cpg-13-vasomotor-sleep-mood-2026.md` §Nonpharmacologic](../markdown/magid-course/acog-cpg-13-vasomotor-sleep-mood-2026.md#nonpharmacologic-interventions)).
  * **Clinical Hypnosis & Stellate Ganglion Block:** Hypnosis reduces VMS frequency/severity in RCTs (`ACOG` conditional); **stellate ganglion block** is a specialist option for severe refractory VMS after breast cancer ([`bms-consensus-non-hormonal-treatments-2026.md` §Other treatments](../markdown/magid-course/bms-consensus-non-hormonal-treatments-2026.md#other-treatments)).
* **Herbal Supplements & Phytoestrogens (Not Recommended):** Unregulated potency/purity (`ACOG 2026` & `BMS 2026`); avoid soy/red clover phytoestrogens in ER+ breast cancer; `Black cohosh` carries rare hepatotoxicity warnings; **`St John's Wort` is a potent `CYP3A4` inducer that dangerously reduces efficacy of `tamoxifen`, anticoagulants, oral contraceptives/HRT, and NK antagonists!** ([`bms-consensus-non-hormonal-treatments-2026.md` §Herbal treatments](../markdown/magid-course/bms-consensus-non-hormonal-treatments-2026.md#herbal-treatments); [`acog-cpg-13-vasomotor-sleep-mood-2026.md` §Clinical Recommendations](../markdown/magid-course/acog-cpg-13-vasomotor-sleep-mood-2026.md#clinical-recommendations-and-evidence-summary)).
