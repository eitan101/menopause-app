## 1. Indications & Clinical Efficacy of Systemic MHT

* **Primary Indications:**
  1. **Vasomotor symptoms (`VMS`)** and **vasomotor-related sleep disturbance** — systemic MHT reduces hot flush frequency by **~75%** (`RR 0.43` vs. placebo; network meta-analysis `mean ratio 0.23` for transdermal E2+P) and severity by **~87%** ([`04-hrt-starting-troubleshooting-stopping.md` §Starting HRT](../markdown/red-whale/04-hrt-starting-troubleshooting-stopping.md#how-effective-is-hrt); [`acog-cpg-13-vasomotor-sleep-mood-2026.md` §Hormone Therapy](../markdown/magid-course/acog-cpg-13-vasomotor-sleep-mood-2026.md#hormone-therapy)).
  2. **POI (`<40y`), Early Menopause (`40–44y`), and Early Surgical Menopause (`BSO`/`RRSO` `<45–50y`)** — strongly indicated **even in asymptomatic women** at least until the average age of natural menopause (`~51–52y`) for bone, cardiovascular, urogenital, and neurocognitive protection ([`13-hrt-primary-prevention-chronic-disease.md`](../markdown/red-whale/13-hrt-primary-prevention-chronic-disease.md#should-hrt-be-used-for-chronic-disease-prevention); [`asrm-eshre-ims-poi-guideline-2024.md` §Section 4](../markdown/magid-course/asrm-eshre-ims-poi-guideline-2024.md#section-4-poi-treatment--hormone-therapy-ht-testosterone-non-hormonal-complementary-lifestyle--puberty-induction-figure-3); [`mht-in-brca-carriers-srebnik.md` §Slide 7–14](../markdown/magid-course/mht-in-brca-carriers-srebnik.md#slide-10-interlace-forest-plot-surgical-menopause-age-hrt-use--incident-cvd--סיכון-קרדיווסקולרי-במנופאוזה-כירורגית-לפי-גיל-ונטילת-hrt)).
  3. **Osteoporosis / Fracture Prevention** in symptomatic postmenopausal women `<60y` or high-fracture-risk women where MHT suits their broader profile ([`10-hrt-and-osteoporosis.md` §Role of HRT](../markdown/red-whale/10-hrt-and-osteoporosis.md#the-role-of-hrt-in-osteoporosis)).
  4. **Perimenopausal Mood Symptoms:** May improve depressive symptoms during the menopausal transition (alone or augmenting SSRIs/CBT), whereas clinical `MDD` requires primary antidepressant/psychotherapy management ([`acog-cpg-13-vasomotor-sleep-mood-2026.md` §Summary of Recommendations](../markdown/magid-course/acog-cpg-13-vasomotor-sleep-mood-2026.md#hormone-therapy)).
* **Not Indicated:** Initiating systemic MHT **solely** for primary prevention of CVD or dementia in asymptomatic postmenopausal women aged `≥50y` (`Grade D`) ([`13-hrt-primary-prevention-chronic-disease.md`](../markdown/red-whale/13-hrt-primary-prevention-chronic-disease.md#uspstf-summary-of-benefits-and-harms-of-hrt-if-given-for-primary-prevention-of-chronic-disease); [`ima-preventive-medicine-guidelines-2022.md` §Postmenopausal HT](../markdown/magid-course/ima-preventive-medicine-guidelines-2022.md#postmenopausal-hormone-therapy-hrt--mht-for-primary-prevention--טיפול-הורמונלי-לאחר-הפסקת-הווסת-כטיפול-מונע-ראשוני)).

## 2. Selecting the Regimen Architecture (Uterus Status & Menopause Stage)

| Uterus & Stage Profile | Required Regimen | Clinical Rules & Transition Triggers |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Prior Total Hysterectomy** *(no residual cervix/endometrium)* | **Estrogen-Only (`ET`)** | **Crucial Exception:** In women with prior **moderate-to-severe endometriosis** (especially with residual peritoneal disease or endometrioma) or **subtotal hysterectomy**, give **continuous combined `E+P`** (at least until natural menopause age or continuously) to prevent reactivation or malignant transformation of residual foci ([`05-hrt-regimens-and-progestogen-doses.md`](../markdown/red-whale/05-hrt-regimens-and-progestogen-doses.md#unopposed-oestrogen-increases-endometrial-cancer-risk); [`menopause-mht-treatment-renana-teomim.md` §Slide 1](../markdown/magid-course/menopause-mht-treatment-renana-teomim.md#slide-1-estrogen-alone-vs-combined-therapy--בחירה--אסטרוגן-בלבד-משולב)) |
| **Intact Uterus — Perimenopause / `<12 months` Since LMP** *(or `<24m` if age `<50y` per UK GEMS)* | **Sequential / Cyclic Combined (`sHRT`)** — **OR** Estrogen + **`52mg LNG-IUS` (`Mirena`)** | Daily continuous estrogen + progestogen for **`12–14 days/month`** (produces predictable monthly withdrawal bleed; avoid `21/7` estrogen-free week regimens like `Progyluton`/`Cyclo-Progynova` if VMS recur during the 7-day break — prefer 28-day continuous estrogen such as `Femoston 1/10` or split transdermal E2 + 12–14d `Utrogestan`/`Duphaston`) ([`05-hrt-regimens-and-progestogen-doses.md` §Initiating HRT](../markdown/red-whale/05-hrt-regimens-and-progestogen-doses.md#initiating-hrt-sequential-or-continuous-progestogen); [`menopause-mht-treatment-renana-teomim.md` §Slide 7](../markdown/magid-course/menopause-mht-treatment-renana-teomim.md#slide-7-sequential-oral-mht-preparations--טיפול-פומי-מחזורי)) |
| **Intact Uterus — Postmenopause (`≥12 months` Amenorrhea)** | **Continuous Combined (`ccHRT`)** — **OR** **`TSEC` (`Duavive`)** — **OR** **`Tibolone` (`Livial`)** | Daily estrogen + daily progestogen (bleed-free goal after initial 3–6m adaptation). **Switch `sHRT` → `ccHRT`** when: **(a)** age **`≥54y`** (~80% postmenopausal), **(b)** **`≥1–2y`** on sequential HRT (and definitely before **`5 years`** on `sHRT` because `>5y` sequential increases endometrial cancer risk!), or **(c)** after `≥12m` amenorrhea ([`05-hrt-regimens-and-progestogen-doses.md` §Endometrial cancer risk](../markdown/red-whale/05-hrt-regimens-and-progestogen-doses.md#endometrial-cancer-risk-continuous-vs-sequential-regimens); [`hebrew-summary-hrt-and-progesterone.md` §Switching](../markdown/red-whale/hebrew-summary-hrt-and-progesterone.md#switching-to-continuous-regimen--נעבור-לטיפול-קבוע)) |

## 3. Route Selection: Transdermal vs. Oral 17β-Estradiol

Oral estrogen undergoes **hepatic first-pass metabolism**, yielding supraphysiological estrone (`E1 > E2`), increasing hepatic procoagulant factors (`VTE`/`stroke`), angiotensinogen, **triglycerides**, **`CRP`**, **`TBG`** (binding thyroxine), and **`SHBG`** (binding free testosterone and lowering libido). **Transdermal 17β-estradiol** (`patches`, `gel`, `spray`) delivers physiological `E2:E1` ratios directly into systemic circulation and avoids first-pass hepatic effects ([`menopause-mht-treatment-renana-teomim.md` §Slides 3–5](../markdown/magid-course/menopause-mht-treatment-renana-teomim.md#slide-5-indications-for-non-oral-estrogen--למי-נעדיף-טיפול-e-שאינו-פומי); [`08-hrt-and-risk-of-vte.md` §How does HRT increase thrombosis](../markdown/red-whale/08-hrt-and-risk-of-vte.md#how-does-hrt-increase-the-risk-of-thrombosis)).

### Clinical Indications Where Transdermal Estradiol Is Preferred or Mandatory
1. **Personal or family history of `VTE` / thrombophilia** *(mandatory transdermal)* ([`08-hrt-and-risk-of-vte.md` §Counselling](../markdown/red-whale/08-hrt-and-risk-of-vte.md#how-should-we-manage-women-with-risk-factors-for-vte)).
2. **Obesity / Overweight (`BMI ≥ 30`, or `≥25–30` with comorbidities)** ([`08-hrt-and-risk-of-vte.md`](../markdown/red-whale/08-hrt-and-risk-of-vte.md#how-should-we-manage-women-with-risk-factors-for-vte)).
3. **Age `≥60y` or Intermediate CV Risk (`10-yr ASCVD / PREVENT 5%–10%`), hypertension, diabetes, smoking, metabolic syndrome, or stabilized Post-MI** ([`slide-cv-risk-evaluation-okelly-2022.md` §Step 3](../markdown/magid-course/slide-cv-risk-evaluation-okelly-2022.md#step-3-evaluate-risk-category-and-select-route); [`bms-hrt-after-myocardial-infarction-2024.md`](../markdown/magid-course/bms-hrt-after-myocardial-infarction-2024.md#practice-points)).
4. **Migraine (with or without aura)** — avoids oral estrogen peaks/troughs that trigger attacks and eliminates oral stroke risk amplification ([`hebrew-summary-menopause-treatment.md` §Estrogen](../markdown/red-whale/hebrew-summary-menopause-treatment.md#estrogen--אסטרוגן); [`asrm-eshre-ims-poi-guideline-2024.md` §Section 4](../markdown/magid-course/asrm-eshre-ims-poi-guideline-2024.md#section-4-poi-treatment--hormone-therapy-ht-testosterone-non-hormonal-complementary-lifestyle--puberty-induction-figure-3)).
5. **Hypertriglyceridemia, gallbladder disease / cholelithiasis, or NAFLD** ([`menopause-mht-treatment-renana-teomim.md` §Slide 5](../markdown/magid-course/menopause-mht-treatment-renana-teomim.md#slide-5-indications-for-non-oral-estrogen--למי-נעדיף-טיפול-e-שאינו-פומי)).
6. **Concurrent `GLP-1`/`GIP` therapy, bariatric surgery, or GI malabsorption** ([`14-drug-interactions-with-hrt.md` §GLP-1](../markdown/red-whale/14-drug-interactions-with-hrt.md#bms-guidance-on-hrt-in-women-on-incretin-based-therapies)).
7. **Concurrent `Levothyroxine` (`Eltroxin`) or `Lamotrigine`** — oral E2 elevates `TBG` (requiring levothyroxine dose adjustment & TSH check at 6–8w) and induces glucuronidation of `lamotrigine` (dropping seizure/mood control levels markedly!) ([`14-drug-interactions-with-hrt.md` §Other drug interactions](../markdown/red-whale/14-drug-interactions-with-hrt.md#hrt-and-other-potential-drug-interactions); [`menopause-mht-treatment-renana-teomim.md` §Slide 5](../markdown/magid-course/menopause-mht-treatment-renana-teomim.md#slide-5-indications-for-non-oral-estrogen--למי-נעדיף-טיפול-e-שאינו-פומי)).
8. **Low Libido / `HSDD`** — preserves free testosterone by avoiding hepatic `SHBG` elevation ([`menopause-mht-treatment-renana-teomim.md` §Slide 5](../markdown/magid-course/menopause-mht-treatment-renana-teomim.md#slide-5-indications-for-non-oral-estrogen--למי-נעדיף-טיפול-e-שאינו-פומי)).

## 4. Hormone Classes: `rBHRT` vs. `CEE` vs. `cBHRT` vs. `Tibolone` vs. `TSEC`

* **Regulated Body-Identical HRT (`rBHRT` — First-Line Standard):** Pharmaceutical-grade **17β-estradiol** + **micronized progesterone** (`Utrogestan`) — plant-derived, molecularly identical to endogenous ovarian hormones, rigorously quality-regulated ([`03-different-types-of-hrt.md` §Regulated bioidentical](../markdown/red-whale/03-different-types-of-hrt.md#regulated-bioidentical-hormone-therapy)).
* **Compounded Bioidentical HRT (`cBHRT` — Strongly Recommended AGAINST):** Custom-compounded creams/pellets/lozenges lack potency standardization, bioequivalence, and endometrial safety evidence, and should **never** be prescribed alongside routine salivary hormone panels ([`03-different-types-of-hrt.md` §Compounded](../markdown/red-whale/03-different-types-of-hrt.md#compounded-bioidentical-hormone-therapy); [`acog-cpg-13-vasomotor-sleep-mood-2026.md` §Hormone Therapy](../markdown/magid-course/acog-cpg-13-vasomotor-sleep-mood-2026.md#hormone-therapy)).
* **Conjugated Equine Estrogens (`CEE`):** Mixture of equine urinary estrogens; considered second-line alone/with MPA due to higher VTE/stroke signal vs. transdermal E2, **except** inside **`Duavive` (`TSEC`: `CEE 0.45 mg + Bazedoxifene 20 mg`)**, which provides a valuable progestogen-free oral option for postmenopausal women with an intact uterus who have severe progestogen intolerance or mastalgia ([`03-different-types-of-hrt.md` §CEE](../markdown/red-whale/03-different-types-of-hrt.md#hrt-containing-conjugated-equine-oestrogens); [`acog-cpg-13-vasomotor-sleep-mood-2026.md` §Hormone Therapy](../markdown/magid-course/acog-cpg-13-vasomotor-sleep-mood-2026.md#hormone-therapy)).
* **Tibolone (`Livial 2.5 mg`):** Synthetic steroid metabolized into estrogenic, progestogenic, and weak Δ4-androgenic metabolites. Improves VMS, bone density, mood, and **libido** with low mastalgia/breast-density impact and bleed-free profile; **start only `≥12 months` post-FMP**, **contraindicated after breast cancer (`LIBERATE` trial showed increased recurrence)**, and **avoid/caution age `>60y` due to increased stroke (`CVA`) risk** ([`03-different-types-of-hrt.md` §Tibolone](../markdown/red-whale/03-different-types-of-hrt.md#tibolone); [`menopause-mht-treatment-renana-teomim.md` §Slide 11](../markdown/magid-course/menopause-mht-treatment-renana-teomim.md#slide-11-continuous-oral-mht-tibolone--טיפול-פומי-רציף-ליביאל)).

## 5. BMS / IMS Systemic 17β-Estradiol Dose-Equivalence Tiers

Matching progestogen dose for endometrial protection requires knowing the **exact estrogen dose tier** ([`05-hrt-regimens-and-progestogen-doses.md` §Oestrogen doses](../markdown/red-whale/05-hrt-regimens-and-progestogen-doses.md#categorisation-of-oestrogen-regimens); [`gems-hrt-troubleshooting-and-availability.md` §Dose Equivalence](../markdown/red-whale/gems-hrt-troubleshooting-and-availability.md#oestradiol-dosing-and-dose-equivalence); [`slide-mht-estrogen-dosing-equivalence-davis-2023.md`](../markdown/magid-course/slide-mht-estrogen-dosing-equivalence-davis-2023.md#systemic-estrogen-dose-tiers-low-vs-mid-range-vs-highest)):

| Preparation (Israeli Trade Name) | Ultra-Low Dose | Low Dose | Standard / Mid Dose | Moderate Dose | High Dose *(often needed in POI / young RRSO)* |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Transdermal Patch (`Evorel`)** | `12.5 mcg` *(¼ of 50)* | `25–37.5 mcg` *(½ of 50)* | **`50 mcg`** | `75 mcg` *(1½ of 50)* | **`100 mcg`** *(or `2×50`)* |
| **Estradiol Gel 0.06% (`Estrogel` / `Oestrogel`)**\* | `0.375 mg` *(½ pump)* | `0.75 mg` *(1 pump / ½ Israeli ruler)* | **`1.5 mg`** *(2 pumps / 1 full Israeli 1.5mg ruler)* | `2.25 mg` *(3 pumps)* | **`3.0 mg`** *(4 pumps / 2 full rulers)* |
| **Estradiol Gel Sachets (`Sandrena`)** | — | `0.5 mg` | **`1.0 mg`** | `1.5–2.0 mg` | **`3.0 mg`** *(off-label)* |
| **Estradiol Skin Spray (`Lenzetto`)** | `1 spray` (`1.53 mg`) | `2 sprays` (`3.06 mg`) | **`3 sprays`** (`4.59 mg`) | `4–5 sprays` *(off-label)* | `6 sprays` *(off-label)* |
| **Oral 17β-Estradiol / E2 Valerate (`Estrofem` / `Progynova`)** | `0.5 mg` *(Eviana / Femoston Conti 0.5)* | `1.0 mg` | **`2.0 mg`** *(Davis 2023 classifies 1mg mid / 2mg high; BMS classifies 1mg low / 2mg standard)* | `3.0 mg` *(off-label)* | **`4.0 mg`** *(off-label)* |

> **\*Israeli Formulary Packaging Note:** In the UK, `Oestrogel` pump-pack dispenses `0.75 mg E2` per pump (`2 pumps = 1.5 mg`). In Israel, `Estrogel`/`Oestrogel` uses a dosing ruler (`2.5 g = 1.5 mg E2` per full measure in [`menopause-mht-treatment-renana-teomim.md` §Slide 16](../markdown/magid-course/menopause-mht-treatment-renana-teomim.md#slide-16-transdermal-estrogen-only-therapy--טיפול-טרנס-דרמלי-אסטרוגן-בלבד) and [`mht-in-brca-carriers-srebnik.md` §Slide 20](../markdown/magid-course/mht-in-brca-carriers-srebnik.md#slide-20-practical-high-dose-mht-regimens-after-early-rrbso--בדכ-יש-צורך-במינוני-טיפול-גבוהים-יותר)) alongside `0.75 mg` actuation pumps — always specify **milligrams of estradiol (`0.75 mg` vs `1.5 mg`)** alongside pump/ruler count!
>
> **Off-Label High-Dose Transdermal Prescribing Rule:** Before exceeding licensed maximum transdermal doses (`>100 mcg` patch or `>3 mg` gel) in non-POI postmenopausal women with persistent VMS, check **serum estradiol** to confirm poor skin absorption (poor absorbers should switch transdermal vehicle—e.g. patch ↔ gel—or oral if low VTE risk, rather than stacking unabsorbed doses) ([`06-hrt-availability-switching-off-licence.md` §Off-licence oestrogen](../markdown/red-whale/06-hrt-availability-switching-off-licence.md#when-might-an-off-licence-dose-of-oestradiol-be-reasonable)).
