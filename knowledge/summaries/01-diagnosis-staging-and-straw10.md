## 1. Hypothalamic-Pituitary-Ovarian (HPO) Physiology & LOOP Cycles

* **Premenopausal Negative Feedback vs. Ovarian Follicular Depletion:** Declining ovarian primordial follicle reserve reduces granulosa-cell secretion of **Inhibin B** and **AMH** (`Anti-Müllerian Hormone`), lifting negative feedback on pituitary **FSH** ([`menopause-staging-and-diagnosis-shaklai.md` §Slide 4](../markdown/magid-course/menopause-staging-and-diagnosis-shaklai.md#slide-4-physiology-of-perimenopause-hpo-axis--פיזיולוגיה-של-פרימנופאוזה); [`01-approaching-and-diagnosing-menopause.md` §Physiology](../markdown/red-whale/01-approaching-and-diagnosing-menopause.md#physiology-of-menopause)).
* **Progesterone Falls First, Estradiol Spikes Erratically:** During perimenopause, anovulatory cycles and luteal-phase insufficiency cause an early, sustained drop in **progesterone**, while elevated FSH drives erratic follicular recruitment and supraphysiological **17β-estradiol (`E2`) surges** before ultimate post-FMP hypoestrogenism ([`menopause-staging-and-diagnosis-shaklai.md` §Slide 7](../markdown/magid-course/menopause-staging-and-diagnosis-shaklai.md#slide-7-progesterone-declines-before-estrogen--פרוגסטרון-יורד-לפני-אסטרוגן)).
* **LOOP (`Luteal Out-Of-Phase`) Cycles (~25% of Perimenopausal Cycles):** Persistently high FSH recruits a second follicular cohort *during* the mid-to-late luteal phase of the preceding cycle, producing extreme estradiol peaks followed by precipitous withdrawal. Clinically, LOOP cycles manifest as shortened (`<21 d`) or prolonged (`>40 d`) cycles, heavy menstrual bleeding (menorrhagia), severe mastalgia, menstrual migraines, endometrial hyperplasia, and fibroid growth ([`menopause-staging-and-diagnosis-shaklai.md` §Slide 9](../markdown/magid-course/menopause-staging-and-diagnosis-shaklai.md#slide-9-estradiol-peaks-in-luteal-out-of-phase-loop-cycles--פיקים-של-אסטרדיול-ב-loop-cycle); [`01-approaching-and-diagnosing-menopause.md` §Physiology](../markdown/red-whale/01-approaching-and-diagnosing-menopause.md#physiology-of-menopause)).
* **KNDy Neuron Thermoregulatory Narrowing:** Hypothalamic kisspeptin/neurokinin B/dynorphin (`KNDy`) neurons hypertrophy as estrogen falls, over-secreting **neurokinin B (`NKB`)** onto `NK3` receptors in the median preoptic thermoregulatory center, narrowing the thermoneutral zone and triggering vasomotor symptoms (`VMS`) ([`acog-cpg-13-vasomotor-sleep-mood-2026.md` §Clinical Overview](../markdown/magid-course/acog-cpg-13-vasomotor-sleep-mood-2026.md#menopausal-symptoms)).

## 3. STRAW+10 Staging System & 2025 IMS Symptom Updates

Menstrual cycle pattern is the **principal criterion** for `STRAW+10` staging (not applicable as sole criterion in PCOS, POI, endometrial ablation, hysterectomy, or Mirena/hormonal contraception) ([`menopause-staging-and-diagnosis-shaklai.md` §Slide 15](../markdown/magid-course/menopause-staging-and-diagnosis-shaklai.md#slide-15-complete-straw10-staging-system-table--טבלת-straw10-המלאה)).

| Stage Code | Stage Name | Principal Menstrual Criterion | Supportive Endocrine / Symptom Profile |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`-3b` / `-3a`** | **Late Reproductive** | Regular cycles (`-3a`: subtle changes in flow or shorter cycle length) | Low AMH/Inhibin B; `-3a` variable early-follicular FSH. **2025 IMS / AMY update:** VMS, sleep disruption, mood shifts, brain fog & `NFLM` ("Not Feeling Like Myself") can already emerge in **`-3a`** despite regular cycles! |
| **`-2`** | **Early Menopausal Transition** | **Persistent `≥7-day` difference** in length of consecutive cycles (recurrence within 10 cycles) | Variable elevated FSH, low AMH/AFC; LOOP cycles, heavy bleeding, cyclical PMS/migraine exacerbation |
| **`-1`** | **Late Menopausal Transition** | **Interval of amenorrhea `≥60 days`** (duration typically `1–3 years`) | Supportive **`FSH > 25 IU/L`**; marked VMS peak & sleep disturbance |
| **`+1a` / `+1b`** | **Early Postmenopause (Years 1–2)** | First 12 months (`+1a`, retrospective FMP window) + second 12 months (`+1b`) | High FSH, low E2; **most likely VMS peak**; rapid trabecular bone loss |
| **`+1c`** | **Early Postmenopause (Years 3–6)** | Years 3–6 post-FMP (`3–6 years` duration) | Stabilized high FSH and low E2; ongoing bone loss; optimal `<10y` CV window for MHT |
| **`+2`** | **Late Postmenopause** | `>6 years` post-FMP through remaining lifespan | Progressive **genitourinary syndrome of menopause (`GSM`)** and somatic aging predominate |

*References:* [`menopause-staging-and-diagnosis-shaklai.md` §Slides 15–17](../markdown/magid-course/menopause-staging-and-diagnosis-shaklai.md#slide-16-updates-since-straw10-2025-ims--amy-study--מה-השתנה-מאז-straw-10), [`acog-cpg-13-vasomotor-sleep-mood-2026.md` §Stages of Menopause](../markdown/magid-course/acog-cpg-13-vasomotor-sleep-mood-2026.md#stages-of-menopause).

## 4. Multisystem Symptom Domains & Differential Red Flags

* **Vasomotor Symptoms (`VMS`):** Hot flushes (typically `1–5 min`), night sweats, chills, clamminess, palpitations; affect **50%–92%** of women, median duration **>7 years** (can begin `>10y` pre-FMP and persist `>10y` post-FMP) ([`acog-cpg-13-vasomotor-sleep-mood-2026.md` §Menopausal Symptoms](../markdown/magid-course/acog-cpg-13-vasomotor-sleep-mood-2026.md#menopausal-symptoms); [`01-approaching-and-diagnosing-menopause.md` §Symptoms](../markdown/red-whale/01-approaching-and-diagnosing-menopause.md#symptoms-of-menopause)).
* **Sleep Disturbance:** Affects `40%–73%`; predominantly **nocturnal awakenings / difficulty maintaining sleep** (`wakefulness after sleep onset`). Distinguish new menopause-onset awakening from pre-existing chronic insomnia, obstructive sleep apnea (`OSA` rises postmenopause — screen with `STOP-BANG`), restless legs, or nocturia ([`acog-cpg-13-vasomotor-sleep-mood-2026.md` §Menopausal Symptoms](../markdown/magid-course/acog-cpg-13-vasomotor-sleep-mood-2026.md#menopausal-symptoms); [`menopause-intake-anamnesis-checklist.md` §Symptom Assessment](../markdown/magid-course/menopause-intake-anamnesis-checklist.md#menopause-symptom-assessment--בירור-תסמינים-מובנה); [`ima-preventive-medicine-guidelines-2022.md` §Sleep Disorders](../markdown/magid-course/ima-preventive-medicine-guidelines-2022.md#epworth-sleepiness-scale--stop-bang-questionnaire--שאלון-אפוורת-לישנוניות-ושאלון-stop-bang-לדום-נשימה-בשינה)).
* **Psychological & Cognitive Symptoms:** Irritability/"short fuse"/rage, anxiety, low mood (~36% depressive symptoms; history of MDD/PMDD/postpartum depression is strongest predictor), word-finding/naming difficulty, poor concentration, and **"brain fog"** ([`01-approaching-and-diagnosing-menopause.md` §Symptoms](../markdown/red-whale/01-approaching-and-diagnosing-menopause.md#symptoms-of-menopause); [`acog-cpg-13-vasomotor-sleep-mood-2026.md` §Menopausal Symptoms](../markdown/magid-course/acog-cpg-13-vasomotor-sleep-mood-2026.md#menopausal-symptoms)).
* **Genitourinary Syndrome of Menopause (`GSM`) & Sexual Function:** Vulvovaginal dryness, burning, dyspareunia, post-coital spotting, urinary urgency/dysuria, recurrent UTIs, and reduced libido (`HSDD`) ([`02-nice-guideline-menopause-2024.md` §GSM](../markdown/red-whale/02-nice-guideline-menopause-2024.md#management-of-genitourinary-symptoms-of-menopause)).
* **Musculoskeletal, Cutaneous, Metabolic & Occupational Impact:** Migratory arthralgia/myalgia ("frozen shoulder"/joint stiffness), accelerated sarcopenia/bone loss, dry skin/hair/nail changes, formication (`גרד, נמלים מתחת לעור`), migraines/vertigo/tinnitus, cardiac palpitations, central adiposity, and **workplace/family functional impairment** (reduced work output, reduced hours/job change/leaving work, close family strain) ([`01-approaching-and-diagnosing-menopause.md` §Symptoms](../markdown/red-whale/01-approaching-and-diagnosing-menopause.md#symptoms-of-menopause); [`israeli-menopause-society-symptoms-quiz.md` §3](../markdown/magid-course/israeli-menopause-society-symptoms-quiz.md#3-complete-19-item-questionnaire-across-6-clinical-domains--טבלת-התסמינים-המלאה)).
* **Critical Differential Diagnoses Not to Miss:** Thyroid dysfunction (including **iatrogenic subclinical hyperthyroidism `TSH 0.05` on levothyroxine** mimicking VMS/insomnia/rage — see Session 5 Case 6!), iron deficiency/anemia (heavy perimenopausal bleeding), pregnancy, hyperprolactinemia, pheochromocytoma/carcinoid/B-symptoms in atypical drenching sweats ([`practical-menopause-course-session5-cases.md` §Case 6](../markdown/magid-course/practical-menopause-course-session5-cases.md#case-6-perimenopausal-51-year-old-with-menstrual-migraines-on-qulipta-suppressed-tsh--family-history-of-breastovarian-cancer--thrombophilia--מקרה-6-page-6); [`menopause-staging-and-diagnosis-shaklai.md` §Slide 18](../markdown/magid-course/menopause-staging-and-diagnosis-shaklai.md#slide-18-when-to-perform-blood-tests--מתי-לבצע-בדיקות-דם)).

## 5. When to Measure FSH (and How Contraception Affects It)

| Patient Scenario | Check Serum FSH? | Clinical Rule & Interpretation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Age `≥45` with typical symptoms / irregular or absent periods** | ❌ **No** | Diagnose **clinically** (`NICE NG23`); perimenopausal FSH fluctuates day-to-day and normal FSH does not exclude perimenopause |
| **Age `40–44` with menopausal symptoms + cycle change** | ✅ **Consider FSH** | Helps confirm early menopause |
| **Age `<40` with oligo/amenorrhea `≥4 months` or VMS (suspected `POI`)** | ✅ **Mandatory** | **2024 ASRM/ESHRE/IMS update:** Single `FSH > 25 IU/L` confirms POI (repeat after `4–6 weeks` **only** if diagnostic uncertainty; older NICE/Red Whale texts still cite 2 samples `4–6w` apart). Exclude pregnancy, check TSH & prolactin; do **not** use AMH as primary diagnostic test |
| **Post-hysterectomy `<45y` (ovaries conserved) or amenorrheic on `POP` / `Implant` / `Mirena` at age `≥50y`** | ✅ **Yes (selectively)** | On progestogen-only contraception at age `≥50`: if **`FSH > 30 IU/L`**, continue contraception for **1 more year** then stop; if `≤ 30 IU/L`, recheck in 1 year or stop at age `55` |
| **Currently taking Combined Hormonal Contraception (`CHC` / COC) or high-dose HRT** | ❌ **Unreliable** | Exogenous ethinylestradiol suppresses pituitary FSH — must withhold CHC (or switch to POP) for `≥2–6 weeks` before valid FSH testing |
| **Poor symptom response on transdermal estradiol** | ⚠️ **Check Serum E2 (not FSH)** | Check serum **oestradiol** to verify transdermal absorption before escalating to off-label high E2 doses (`FSH` does not reliably guide HRT dose titration) |
