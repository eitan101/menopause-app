## 1. Premature Ovarian Insufficiency (`POI`, Age `<40`): 2024 ASRM / ESHRE / IMS Guideline & NEJM Synthesis

* **Updated Epidemiology & Diagnostic Criteria (`2024 ASRM/ESHRE/CREWHIRL/IMS` vs. `NEJM 2023`):**
  * **Prevalence:** Updated globally from 1% to **`3.5%–3.7%`** of women before age 40 ([`asrm-eshre-ims-poi-guideline-2024.md` §Section 1](../markdown/magid-course/asrm-eshre-ims-poi-guideline-2024.md#section-1-introduction-to-poi--nomenclature-definition-prevalence--risk-factors)).
  * **Streamlined 2024 Diagnostic Rule (`[Rec 9–13]`):** Diagnose non-iatrogenic POI with **oligo/amenorrhea for `≥4 months`** AND a **SINGLE elevated serum `FSH > 25 IU/L`** (after excluding pregnancy, thyroid disease, and hyperprolactinemia, with COCs/HRT withheld prior to blood draw). **Repeat `FSH` after `4–6 weeks` ONLY if diagnostic uncertainty exists** (updating the older two-test rule described in `NEJM 2023` and `NICE`) ([`asrm-eshre-ims-poi-guideline-2024.md` §Section 2](../markdown/magid-course/asrm-eshre-ims-poi-guideline-2024.md#section-2-diagnosis-of-poi--investigation-of-etiology-figure-2); [`nejm-clinical-practice-poi-stuenkel-2023.md` §Diagnosis and Evaluation](../markdown/magid-course/nejm-clinical-practice-poi-stuenkel-2023.md#diagnosis-and-evaluation)).
  * **Role of `AMH` (`[Rec 14–15]`):** Do **NOT** use `AMH` as the primary diagnostic test for POI.
* **Mandatory Etiologic & Baseline Screening Workup in Non-Iatrogenic POI:**
  1. **Chromosomal Karyotype (`[Rec 17–21]`):** Perform in **ALL women with non-iatrogenic POI regardless of age**; if `Y`-chromosome material is present, perform gonadectomy due to gonadoblastoma risk.
  2. **Fragile X (`FMR1`) Premutation Testing (`[Rec 22–24]`):** Offer to **ALL women with non-iatrogenic POI** with pre-test counseling (`55–200` CGG repeats = `FXPOI` toxic mRNA gain-of-function, `FXTAS` tremor-ataxia risk in family, and risk of expansion to `>200` full Fragile X mutation in offspring; note `>200` full mutation itself does *not* cause FXPOI!) ([`nejm-clinical-practice-poi-stuenkel-2023.md` §Figure 2](../markdown/magid-course/nejm-clinical-practice-poi-stuenkel-2023.md#figure-2-fragile-x-premutation-carrier-identification-and-implications-in-women-with-primary-ovarian-insufficiency); [`practical-menopause-course-session5-cases.md` §Case 2](../markdown/magid-course/practical-menopause-course-session5-cases.md#case-2-asymptomatic-premature-menopause-fragile-x-premutation-with-mht-side-effects--מקרה-2-page-2)).
  3. **Targeted POI `NGS` Gene Panel (`[Rec 27–28]`):** Consider where available if karyotype and `FMR1` are normal.
  4. **Autoimmune Screening (`[Rec 31–37]`):** Check **21-hydroxylase antibodies (`21-OHAb`)** to screen for occult adrenal autoimmunity / Addison disease (refer to endocrinology if positive!) + **`TSH` and `TPO-Ab`** (do *not* test anti-ovarian antibodies).
  5. **Baseline `DXA` Bone Density Scan (`[Rec 54–63]`):** Perform at diagnosis; repeat in `2–5 years` if low BMD/osteoporosis, + annual cardiometabolic (`BP`, `BMI`, smoking, lipids/diabetes) review ([`asrm-eshre-ims-poi-guideline-2024.md` §Section 3](../markdown/magid-course/asrm-eshre-ims-poi-guideline-2024.md#section-3-sequelae-of-poi--life-expectancy-fertility-pregnancy-bone-muscle-cardiovascular-psychological-sexual--neurological-health)).

## 2. Hormone Therapy & Fertility / Contraception Nuances in POI

* **Physiological 17β-Estradiol Replacement Over COCs (`[Rec 92–114]`):**
  * **Promptly initiate systemic HT in ALL women with POI (including completely asymptomatic women!)** and continue **at least until age `50–52`** to prevent premature cardiovascular mortality, osteoporosis, dementia, and urogenital atrophy ([`asrm-eshre-ims-poi-guideline-2024.md` §Section 4](../markdown/magid-course/asrm-eshre-ims-poi-guideline-2024.md#section-4-poi-treatment--hormone-therapy-ht-testosterone-non-hormonal-complementary-lifestyle--puberty-induction-figure-3); [`practical-menopause-course-session5-cases.md` §Case 2](../markdown/magid-course/practical-menopause-course-session5-cases.md#case-2-asymptomatic-premature-menopause-fragile-x-premutation-with-mht-side-effects--מקרה-2-page-2)).
  * **Physiological 17β-estradiol is superior to ethinylestradiol `COCs`** for bone mineral density accrual and lower BP/metabolic impact ([`asrm-eshre-ims-poi-guideline-2024.md` §Section 4](../markdown/magid-course/asrm-eshre-ims-poi-guideline-2024.md#section-4-poi-treatment--hormone-therapy-ht-testosterone-non-hormonal-complementary-lifestyle--puberty-induction-figure-3); [`nejm-clinical-practice-poi-stuenkel-2023.md` §Table 1](../markdown/magid-course/nejm-clinical-practice-poi-stuenkel-2023.md#table-1-representative-guideline-recommendations-for-hrt-in-women-with-primary-ovarian-insufficiency)).
  * **Higher Physiological Estrogen Doses Required:** Standard replacement in POI is **`100 mcg/d` transdermal E2 patch** (`75–100 mcg/d`), **`1.5–3 mg/d` E2 gel**, or **`2 mg/d` (up to `2–4 mg/d`) oral 17β-estradiol**, paired with proportional micronized progesterone (`200 mg × 12–14 d/mo` cyclic — which allows prompt recognition of missed withdrawal bleeds if pregnancy is desired! — or continuous `100–200 mg/d`), dydrogesterone (`10 mg`), or `52mg LNG-IUS`.
* **Spontaneous Ovulation (`1.5%–10%`) & Contraception Trap in POI:**
  * Standard HRT **does NOT prevent ovulation**. Even standard `21/7` COCs can occasionally fail in POI because endogenous FSH rebounds during the 7-day pill-free interval! When contraception is desired in POI, use **`52mg LNG-IUS` (`Mirena`) + systemic 17β-estradiol**, **continuous/extended-regimen COC** (eliminating hormone-free intervals), or barrier methods ([`nejm-clinical-practice-poi-stuenkel-2023.md` §Contraception](../markdown/magid-course/nejm-clinical-practice-poi-stuenkel-2023.md#contraception)).

## 3. Perimenopausal Contraception & Stopping Rules (Ages 40–55)

Fertility declines sharply after age 40, **but spontaneous pregnancy remains possible until menopause is confirmed** (`2 years` of natural amenorrhea if `<50y`, or **`1 year` of natural amenorrhea if `≥50y`**, or universal stopping age **`55y`**) ([`hebrew-summary-perimenopause-contraception.md`](../markdown/red-whale/hebrew-summary-perimenopause-contraception.md); [`acog-cpg-13-vasomotor-sleep-mood-2026.md` §Patient Counseling](../markdown/magid-course/acog-cpg-13-vasomotor-sleep-mood-2026.md#patient-counseling-and-education)).

| Contraceptive Method | Age Cutoff / Duration Rule | Overlap with MHT (`HRT`) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`52 mg LNG-IUS` (`Mirena`)** | • **For contraception alone:** If inserted at **age `≥45y`**, effective for contraception until **age `55`** (or `7–8y` per contraceptive license)<br>• **CRITICAL MHT RULE:** When used as the **progestogen component of MHT**, **MUST be replaced every `5 years`** regardless of age! | ✅ **Dual-purpose gold standard:** Provides contraception + heavy-bleeding control + MHT endometrial protection across all E2 doses |
| **Copper IUD (`Cu-IUD`)** | If inserted at **age `≥40y`**, may remain in place until menopause (`1y` amenorrhea `≥50y`, `2y` `<50y`) or age `55` | ❌ Worsens heavy/painful perimenopausal bleeding; provides **no** endometrial protection if MHT is started (still need oral/patch progestogen) |
| **Combined Hormonal Contraception (`CHC` / COC)** | Eligible **up to age `50y`** ONLY in non-smokers without VTE, migraine with aura, HTN, obesity (`BMI ≥35`), or CVD risk (`≤30 mcg` EE or E2/E4 pills). **Switch off CHC at age `50`!** | Treats VMS/irregular bleeding & protects bone `<50y`, **but** do not combine alongside HRT; if VMS occur in pill-free week (see Session 5 Case 5), either shorten break `<50y` or **switch to MHT + non-estrogen contraception at `≥50y`** |
| **Progestogen-Only Pill (`POP`: `Cerazette`, `Slynd`) or `Implant`** | Safe **up to age `55y`** even when estrogens are contraindicated | ❌ **Does NOT count as MHT progestogen!** Can be co-prescribed alongside a **sequential combined HRT** pack (or switch to `Mirena` + E2) |
| **Depot MPA (`Depo-Provera`)** | **Switch to another method by age `50y`** (reduces bone mineral density and raises VTE/metabolic concerns) | ❌ Not used as MHT progestogen ([`10-hrt-and-osteoporosis.md` §DMPA](../markdown/red-whale/10-hrt-and-osteoporosis.md#drug-dilemma-what-about-medroxyprogesterone-acetate-depot-contraceptive-injection)) |

### FSH Protocol to Confirm Menopause on Progestogen-Only Contraception at Age `≥50y`
For amenorrheic women **aged `50–54`** taking `POP`, subdermal `implant`, or `LNG-IUS` who wish to know if they can stop contraception before age 55 ([`hebrew-summary-perimenopause-contraception.md` §FSH Testing](../markdown/red-whale/hebrew-summary-perimenopause-contraception.md#fsh-testing-and-stopping-contraception--בדיקת-fsh-והפסקת-אמצעי-מניעה); [`01-approaching-and-diagnosing-menopause.md` §Hormonal contraception](../markdown/red-whale/01-approaching-and-diagnosing-menopause.md#can-fsh-be-accurately-measured-in-women-on-hormonal-contraception)):
* Check single serum **`FSH`**:
  * **`FSH > 30 IU/L`** ➔ Continue contraception for **1 more year**, then **stop** (no further FSH needed).
  * **`FSH ≤ 30 IU/L`** ➔ Continue contraception and **recheck FSH in 1 year** (or continue until age **55**, when natural conception is exceptionally rare and contraception can be stopped).
