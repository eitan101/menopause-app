## 1. Consolidated Israeli Menopause Drug Formulary

Cross-referenced from [`menopause-mht-treatment-renana-teomim.md` §Slides 7–20](../markdown/magid-course/menopause-mht-treatment-renana-teomim.md#slide-7-sequential-oral-mht-preparations--טיפול-פומי-מחזורי), [`slide-systemic-mht-options-israeli-annotations-2026.md`](../markdown/magid-course/slide-systemic-mht-options-israeli-annotations-2026.md), and [`hebrew-summary-menopause-treatment.md`](../markdown/red-whale/hebrew-summary-menopause-treatment.md):

| Regimen Category | Israeli Trade Name | Active Ingredients & Strengths | Schedule & Key Israeli Prescribing Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Transdermal Estrogen Only** | **`Evorel 50` / `Evorel 100`** | 17β-estradiol `50` or `100 mcg/24h` matrix patch | Replace **twice weekly**; matrix patch **can be cut** (`½` of `Evorel 50` = `25 mcg`, `1½` = `75 mcg`) |
| **Transdermal Estrogen Only** | **`Estrogel` / `Oestrogel 0.06%`** | 17β-estradiol percutaneous gel (`0.75 mg/pump` or `1.5 mg/full ruler`) | Daily skin application; always specify **mg dose** (`0.75 mg` low, `1.5 mg` standard, `2.25–3.0 mg` moderate/high) |
| **Transdermal Estrogen Only** | **`Sandrena`** *(where stocked)* | 17β-estradiol `0.5 mg` / `1.0 mg` gel sachets | 1 sachet daily (`1.0 mg` standard) |
| **Oral Estrogen Only** | **`Estrofem`** / **`Progynova`** | 17β-estradiol `1 mg`, `2 mg` / E2 valerate `2 mg` | 1 tablet daily (`Estrofem 2 mg` or `3 mg` often used in young `BRCA`/`POI` if oral chosen) |
| **Separate Progestogen** | **`Utrogestan`** / **`Gepretix`** | Micronized progesterone `100 mg`, `200 mg` | Bedtime oral (`100 mg` daily ccHRT or `200 mg × 12–14 d` sHRT; escalate to `200 mg` cc / `300 mg` seq with high E2). **Avoid in peanut/soy allergy** |
| **Separate Progestogen** | **`Duphaston`** | Dydrogesterone `10 mg` | Oral (`5–10 mg/d` ccHRT or `10 mg × 14 d` sHRT; non-sedating, safe in soy allergy) |
| **Intrauterine Progestogen** | **`Mirena`** | Levonorgestrel `52 mg` IUS (`20 mcg/d` initial) | **Replace every `5 years` for MHT endometrial protection** (covers all E2 doses + `GLP-1` + heavy bleeding + contraception) |
| **Sequential Combined Oral** | **`Femoston 1/10` & `2/10`** | `1` or `2 mg` E2 (28d continuous) + `10 mg` dydrogesterone (last 14d) | Preferred oral sequential pack (**no estrogen-free week**, preventing hormone-free VMS!) |
| **Sequential Combined Oral** | **`Novofem`** / **`Trisequens`** / **`Progyluton`** / **`Cyclo-Progynova`** | `Novofem` (`1mg E2 + 1mg NETA`); `Progyluton`/`Cyclo-Progynova` (`2mg EV + norgestrel`, **21/7**) | Note: `Progyluton` & `Cyclo-Progynova` have a **7-day hormone-free interval** where VMS/migraines may recur; none provide contraception! |
| **Continuous Combined Patch** | **`Evorel Conti`** | `50 mcg/24h` E2 + `170 mcg/24h` NETA | Replace **twice weekly**; convenient single-patch `ccHRT` (can cut patch to adjust dose) |
| **Continuous Combined Oral** | **`Femoston Conti`** (`1/5`, `0.5/2.5`) | E2 `1 mg` / dydrogesterone `5 mg` (or `0.5 / 2.5 mg`) | High breast/metabolic safety among oral combined pills (`0.5/2.5` is ultra-low — avoid in young POI/RRSO) |
| **Continuous Combined Oral** | **`Activelle`** / **`Eviana`** | E2 `1 mg + 0.5 mg` NETA (`Activelle`) / `0.5 mg + 0.1 mg` NETA (`Eviana`) | Strong bone & bleeding control; `NETA` may cause androgenic side effects |
| **Continuous Combined Oral** | **`Angeliq`** | E2 `1 mg` + drospirenone `2 mg` | Anti-androgenic + anti-mineralocorticoid (lowers BP/bloating); **check K⁺/renal function & ACEi/ARB co-use** |
| **Progestogen-Free `TSEC` / `STEAR`** | **`Duavive`** / **`Livial`** | `CEE 0.45 mg + Bazedoxifene 20 mg` / `Tibolone 2.5 mg` | `Duavive`: severe progestogen intolerance/mastalgia; `Livial`: improves libido, `≥1y` post-FMP, **caution `>60y` (stroke)** |
| **Local GSM & Non-Hormonal** | **`Vagifem 10mcg`**, **`Ovestin`**, **`Intrarosa 6.5mg`**, **`Veoza 45mg`** | Local E2 / Estriol / Prasterone / Fezolinetant (`NK3i`) | `Vagifem`/`Ovestin` 2w nightly then 2×/wk; `Veoza 45mg` od with baseline + 1, 2, 3 (and 6, 9) mo `LFTs` |

## 2. Clinical Pearls from the 9 Magid Course Case Discussions (`Session 5`)

Real-world Israeli primary-care edge cases documented in [`practical-menopause-course-session5-cases.md`](../markdown/magid-course/practical-menopause-course-session5-cases.md) that any robust CDSS must handle gracefully:

1. **Suboptimal Control on Fixed Low/Standard Oral Pill (`Case 1`):** Switching `Activelle` → **transdermal E2 (up-titrated) + bedtime `Utrogestan`** simultaneously resolves daytime VMS, 3 AM awakenings, and elevated LDL/paternal IHD risk (+ add local vaginal E2 for residual GSM).
2. **Asymptomatic `FXPOI` wanting to quit due to side effects (`Case 2`):** Never let a 41-year-old POI patient abandon HT because of bleeding on `Femoston Conti` or patch rash on `Evorel Conti` — switch to **`Estrogel` + cyclic progestogen or `Mirena`** and counsel on life-expectancy/CVD/bone protection until age `~52`.
3. **False Breast Contraindication Fears (`Case 3`):** Maternal breast cancer at age 70 (negative `BRCA`) + personal **benign breast biopsy without atypia** do **not** contraindicate MHT.
4. **`Utrogestan` GI Intolerance + Post-Switch Bleeding in `BMI 34` (`Case 4`):** Distinguish excipient/metformin diarrhea from hormonal side effects, and apply minor endometrial risk factor rules (`BMI 30–39` + prediabetes) + 4-week post-cessation check.
5. **Age >50 Still Taking `COC` (`Meliane`) with Pill-Free Week VMS (`Case 5`):** Actively transition healthy women `≥50y` off ethinylestradiol `COCs` onto physiological MHT (+ `POP`/`Mirena`/barrier until menopause confirmed).
6. **Iatrogenic Hyperthyroidism (`TSH 0.05` on `Eltroxin`) Mimicking Perimenopause (`Case 6`):** Always flag **suppressed `TSH`** on levothyroxine before attributing sweats, rage, and insomnia solely to perimenopause!
7. **Late Initiation at Age 62 / 11 Years Post-FMP (`Case 7`):** Outside `<60y / <10y` window with smoking + HTN + dyslipidemia -> calculate `10-yr ASCVD/PREVENT` risk, prioritize `Veoza`/SSRIs/CBT and smoking/statin optimization, or use ultra-low transdermal E2 only via shared decision-making if low/intermediate risk.
8. **Early Transition Heavy Bleeding at Age 47 vs. Late Persistent Heavy Bleeding at Age 53 (`Case 8`):** `52mg Mirena` (± transdermal E2) is transformative, after age-appropriate endometrial evaluation.
9. **Expired `Mirena` (`8 Years` In Situ) + Solitary Kidney (`Case 9`):** Track **`Mirena` insertion date** (`>5 years` = expired endometrial protection causing irregular bleeding!) and **contraindicate `Angeliq`/drospirenone in solitary kidney / CKD**.
