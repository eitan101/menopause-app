## 1. Structured First-Visit Menopause Anamnesis (Stage 1 Intake)

Per the Magid Practical Menopause Course intake protocol and Red Whale pre-HRT assessment guidance ([`menopause-intake-anamnesis-checklist.md`](../markdown/magid-course/menopause-intake-anamnesis-checklist.md); [`04-hrt-starting-troubleshooting-stopping.md` §Starting HRT](../markdown/red-whale/04-hrt-starting-troubleshooting-stopping.md#starting-hrt)), initial assessment captures six core clinical domains:

1. **Demographics, Vitals & Lifestyle:** Age, occupation, marital/psychosocial context, height/weight/`BMI` + waist circumference (`>88 cm` central adiposity threshold in women), seated clinic blood pressure (`BP`), smoking (accelerates menopause by `1–2 years` and worsens osteoporosis), alcohol intake, Mediterranean nutrition quality, and aerobic/resistance physical activity ([`menopause-intake-anamnesis-checklist.md` §Demographics](../markdown/magid-course/menopause-intake-anamnesis-checklist.md#demographics-medical-history-and-lifestyle--פרטים-אישיים-רקע-רפואי-ואורח-חיים); [`ima-preventive-medicine-guidelines-2022.md` §Smoking & Women's Health](../markdown/magid-course/ima-preventive-medicine-guidelines-2022.md#smoking-harms--impact-on-womens-health-and-early-menopause--נזקי-העישון-ובריאות-האישה)).
2. **Obstetric, Gynaecological & Reproductive History:** Gravidity/parity (`G/P/Ab`), fertility treatments, age at menarche, history of `PMS`/`PMDD` or postpartum depression (predicts perimenopausal mood vulnerability and progestogen sensitivity), `PCOS`, endometriosis, fibroids/polyps, prior endometrial sampling (`Pipelle` / hysteroscopy), hysterectomy and/or bilateral salpingo-oophorectomy (`BSO`/`RRSO`), current bleeding pattern (`LMP`/`FMP`, cycle length variation, heaviness), and contraception history ([`menopause-intake-anamnesis-checklist.md` §Obstetric & Gynaecological](../markdown/magid-course/menopause-intake-anamnesis-checklist.md#obstetric-gynaecological-and-menopausal-history--אנמנזה-מיילדותית-וגינקולוגית); [`acog-cpg-13-vasomotor-sleep-mood-2026.md` §Menopausal Symptoms](../markdown/magid-course/acog-cpg-13-vasomotor-sleep-mood-2026.md#menopausal-symptoms)).
3. **Women-Specific Cardiovascular Risk Enhancers:** Explicitly screen for **preeclampsia / gestational hypertension**, **gestational diabetes (`GDM`)**, **preterm delivery**, **`PCOS`**, **early/premature menopause (`<45y` / `<40y`)**, and **systemic autoimmune disease** (e.g., SLE, rheumatoid arthritis) ([`menopause-intake-anamnesis-checklist.md` §Women-Specific CV Risk](../markdown/magid-course/menopause-intake-anamnesis-checklist.md#women-specific-cardiovascular-risk-enhancers--רשימת-גורמי-הסיכון-למחלה-קרדיווסקולרית); [`slide-cv-risk-evaluation-okelly-2022.md` §Step 2](../markdown/magid-course/slide-cv-risk-evaluation-okelly-2022.md#step-2-perform-ascvd-risk-assessment-and-exclude-ht-contraindications); [`ima-preventive-medicine-guidelines-2022.md` §Female Reproductive CVD Risk](../markdown/magid-course/ima-preventive-medicine-guidelines-2022.md#diabetes-obesity-metabolic-syndrome--female-reproductive-cvd-risk-factors--סוכרת-השמנה-תסמונת-מטבולית-וסיבוכי-היריון-כגורמי-סיכון-בנשים)).
4. **Family History & Hereditary Cancer Genetics:** Maternal/sibling age at menopause, premature first-degree `CVD` (`men <55y`, `women <65y`), `VTE`/thrombophilia, diabetes, osteoporosis/parental hip fracture, breast/ovarian/endometrial/colorectal cancer, ancestry (Ashkenazi Jewish `1:40` `BRCA1/2` carrier prevalence), and **`BRCA1/2` or `Lynch` syndrome** testing status ([`menopause-intake-anamnesis-checklist.md` §Family History](../markdown/magid-course/menopause-intake-anamnesis-checklist.md#family-history-and-genetic-screening--רקע-משפחתי-וגנטי); [`ima-preventive-medicine-guidelines-2022.md` §BRCA1/2 Screening](../markdown/magid-course/ima-preventive-medicine-guidelines-2022.md#genetic-counseling--brca12-screening-including-israeli-basket-ashkenazi-screening--ייעוץ-גנטי-בדיקות-brca12-וניהול-נשאיות)).
5. **Medications, Supplements & Allergies:** Incretin mimetics (`GLP-1`/`GIP`: semaglutide, tirzepatide), antiepileptics (`lamotrigine`, enzyme inducers `carbamazepine`/`phenytoin`), `tamoxifen`/aromatase inhibitors, `levothyroxine`, `ACEi`/`ARBs`/potassium-sparing diuretics (caution with `Angeliq`/drospirenone), herbal supplements (`St John's wort`), and **peanut/soy allergy** (contraindication to oral/vaginal `Utrogestan` capsules containing soy lecithin) ([`14-drug-interactions-with-hrt.md`](../markdown/red-whale/14-drug-interactions-with-hrt.md#hrt-and-glp-1-mimetics-and-other-incretin-based-therapies); [`menopause-mht-treatment-renana-teomim.md` §Slide 10 & Slide 19](../markdown/magid-course/menopause-mht-treatment-renana-teomim.md#slide-10-continuous-oral-mht-estradiol--drospirenone--טיפול-פומי-רציף-אנגליק)).
6. **Baseline Symptom & Patient-Priority Quantification (`IMS 19-Item Scale`):** Structured `0–4` rating across **6 domains and 19 items** (`Vasomotor` [hot flushes/sweats, migraines/vertigo/tinnitus — rated on `Severity`, `Frequency`, and `Importance`], `Psychological & Cognitive` [low mood, irritability, anxiety/panic, exhaustion/`brain fog`], `Somatic` [sleep, cardiac palpitations, cycle change, joint/muscle pain, formication, skin/hair/nails], `Urogenital & Sexuality` [desire/satisfaction, urinary symptoms, vaginal dryness/dyspareunia], `Metabolic` [weight/central adiposity], and `Social & Occupational` [work performance, reduced job scope/stopping work, close family strain]), pairing **`עוצמה` (`Severity 0–4`)** with **`דרגת חשיבות` (`Patient Importance 0–4`)** at **Baseline (`לפני מתן טיפול`)**, **3 months (`כעבור שלושה חודשים`)**, and **6 months (`כעבור שישה חודשים`)** ([`israeli-menopause-society-symptoms-quiz.md` §1–3](../markdown/magid-course/israeli-menopause-society-symptoms-quiz.md#1-purpose-structure--longitudinal-timepoints--מבנה-השאלון-וצירי-המעקב); [`menopause-intake-anamnesis-checklist.md` §Symptom Assessment](../markdown/magid-course/menopause-intake-anamnesis-checklist.md#menopause-symptom-assessment--בירור-תסמינים-מובנה)).

## 2. Israeli Preventive Medicine & Health Basket (`סל הבריאות`) Screening Rules for Midlife Women

Based on the **Israeli Medical Association (IMA) Preventive Medicine Task Force Guidelines (Oct 2022)** ([`ima-preventive-medicine-guidelines-2022.md`](../markdown/magid-course/ima-preventive-medicine-guidelines-2022.md)):

| Domain | Israeli Guideline & Health Basket Rule (`Ages 40–64` & `65+`) | Layer 2 Section Anchor |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Breast Cancer (Average Risk)** | Biennial mammography **ages `50–74`** (`כל שנתיים`). Ages `40–49`: shared decision-making (not routine). Age `≥75`: individualize if life expectancy `>10y` | [`ima-preventive-medicine-guidelines-2022.md` §Breast Cancer](../markdown/magid-course/ima-preventive-medicine-guidelines-2022.md#breast-cancer-screening--איתור-מוקדם-של-סרטן-השד-ממוגרפיה-שד-סמיך-dbt-us-mri) |
| **Breast Cancer (Increased Risk)** | Annual mammography from **age `40`** (or `10y` before youngest affected 1st-degree relative; also chest radiation age `10–30` or biopsy with atypia). Note: simple benign biopsy *without* atypia or single older maternal BC (e.g., age 70 with negative genetics) is **not** a contraindication to MHT | [`ima-preventive-medicine-guidelines-2022.md` §Breast Cancer](../markdown/magid-course/ima-preventive-medicine-guidelines-2022.md#breast-cancer-screening--איתור-מוקדם-של-סרטן-השד-ממוגרפיה-שד-סמיך-dbt-us-mri), [`practical-menopause-course-session5-cases.md` §Case 3](../markdown/magid-course/practical-menopause-course-session5-cases.md#case-3-severe-vms-at-age-49-with-prior-weight-loss-wegovy-maternal-breast-cancer-at-70--benign-breast-biopsy--מקרה-3-page-3) |
| **`BRCA1/2` Population Screening** | **Funded in Israeli Health Basket (since 2020) for ALL women age `≥25` of full or partial Ashkenazi Jewish ancestry** (`14` founder mutations) **without requiring pre-test genetic counseling** | [`ima-preventive-medicine-guidelines-2022.md` §BRCA1/2 Screening](../markdown/magid-course/ima-preventive-medicine-guidelines-2022.md#genetic-counseling--brca12-screening-including-israeli-basket-ashkenazi-screening--ייעוץ-גנטי-בדיקות-brca12-וניהול-נשאיות), [`mht-in-brca-carriers-srebnik.md` §Slide 2](../markdown/magid-course/mht-in-brca-carriers-srebnik.md#slide-2-why-every-clinician-must-know-brca-management--למה-צריך-ללמוד-על-brca) |
| **Cervical Cancer (`Pap` / `hrHPV`)** | `hrHPV` / Pap every **`3–5 years`** at **ages `25–65`** (applies equally to WSW). Stop `>65` if adequate negative prior screening and low risk | [`ima-preventive-medicine-guidelines-2022.md` §Cervical Cancer](../markdown/magid-course/ima-preventive-medicine-guidelines-2022.md#ovarian--cervical-cancer-screening-pap-smear--hrhpv--איתור-מוקדם-של-סרטן-השחלה-וסרטן-צוואר-הרחם) |
| **Routine Pelvic Exam / Ovarian US** | **Recommended AGAINST (Grade D)** in asymptomatic non-carrier women (`TVS` and `CA-125` do not reduce mortality and cause unnecessary surgery); investigate any abnormal/postmenopausal bleeding promptly | [`ima-preventive-medicine-guidelines-2022.md` §Routine Pelvic Exam](../markdown/magid-course/ima-preventive-medicine-guidelines-2022.md#routine-pelvic-examination--endometrialovarian-screening-in-healthy-women--ביצוע-בדיקה-נרתיקית-שגרתית-באישה-בריאה) |
| **Colorectal Cancer** | Annual **`FIT` (`דם סמוי בצואה`) ages `50–74`** (or colonoscopy every `10y`; 1st-degree relative -> colonoscopy every `5y` from age `40` or `10y` earlier) | [`ima-preventive-medicine-guidelines-2022.md` §Colorectal Cancer](../markdown/magid-course/ima-preventive-medicine-guidelines-2022.md#colorectal-cancer-screening--איתור-מוקדם-של-סרטן-המעי-הגס-דם-סמוי-בצואה-וקולונוסקופיה) |
| **Osteoporosis (`DXA` & `FRAX`)** | Universal `DXA` at **age `≥65`** (USPSTF) and **funded in Israeli Health Basket from age `60`**; ages **`50–59`** if risk factors / elevated `FRAX` (and **at diagnosis** in `POI` / early menopause `<45y`) | [`ima-preventive-medicine-guidelines-2022.md` §Osteoporosis DXA](../markdown/magid-course/ima-preventive-medicine-guidelines-2022.md#osteoporosis-definitions-frax--dxa-screening-including-israeli-health-basket--אבחון-אוסטיאופורוזיס-מודל-frax-ובדיקת-סקר-לצפיפות-עצם-dxa), [`10-hrt-and-osteoporosis.md` §Who needs DXA](../markdown/red-whale/10-hrt-and-osteoporosis.md#who-needs-dxa-scan) |
| **Cardiometabolic (`BP`, `Lipids`, `Lp(a)`, `Glucose`)** | `BP` every `1–2y` (`≥40y`, annual if `≥130/85` or risk factors); fasting/non-fasting lipids every `5y` (`≥35–40y`) + **one-time lifetime `Lipoprotein(a)`**; `FPG` or `HbA1c` every `3y` in ages `35–70` with `BMI ≥25` (or prior `GDM`/`PCOS`) | [`ima-preventive-medicine-guidelines-2022.md` §Table Zayin 40–64](../markdown/magid-course/ima-preventive-medicine-guidelines-2022.md#table-zayin-ages-4064--טבלה-ז-רפואה-מונעת-בגיל-64-40-שנים) |
| **Thyroid (`TSH`)** | Not routine in completely asymptomatic adults, **but low clinical threshold** in midlife fatigue, mood/sleep change, dyslipidemia, autoimmune history, POI, or patients taking `levothyroxine` | [`ima-preventive-medicine-guidelines-2022.md` §Controversial Screening](../markdown/magid-course/ima-preventive-medicine-guidelines-2022.md#routine-gynecologic-exam-skin-screening-ecg--thyroid--tsh-screening--בדיקה-גינקולוגית-שגרתית-עור-אקגמבחן-מאמץ-ותפקודי-בלוטת-המגן-tsh) |
| **IPV, Depression & Vaccines** | Proactive inquiry for **intimate partner violence (`IPV`)** (`LIVES` framework) + `PHQ-2` depression screen; vaccines: annual **Influenza**, **Shingrix** (`≥50y`), **`Td`/`Tdap`** q10y, **Pneumococcal** (`≥65y` or high-risk) | [`ima-preventive-medicine-guidelines-2022.md` §IPV](../markdown/magid-course/ima-preventive-medicine-guidelines-2022.md#domestic-violence-intimate-partner-violence-ipv--sexual-assault--פרק-19-אלימות-במשפחה-ופגיעה-מינית), [`ima-preventive-medicine-guidelines-2022.md` §Immunizations](../markdown/magid-course/ima-preventive-medicine-guidelines-2022.md#table-4-recommended-adult-immunization-schedule--טבלה-4-לוח-החיסונים-המומלצים-למבוגרים) |

## 3. Contraindications to Systemic MHT vs. Nuanced Specialist Scenarios

| Category | Clinical Condition | Management & Pathway |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Absolute / Standard Contraindications to Systemic MHT** | • Current or past **breast cancer** *(systemic MHT contraindicated; local vaginal estrogen may be considered selectively — see Topic 08)*<br>• Untreated / high-grade **endometrial cancer** or uterine sarcoma *(early stage I–II may be considered jointly with gynecologic oncologist per ACOG)*<br>• **Undiagnosed abnormal vaginal / postmenopausal bleeding**<br>• **Active / acute VTE** (`DVT`/`PE`) or acute arterial thromboembolism (`stroke`/`TIA`/unstable angina)<br>• **Active decompensated liver disease** / acute porphyria<br>• Known or suspected **pregnancy** | **Block systemic MHT** at Stage 1 Gate; route to urgent investigation (for undiagnosed bleeding/liver workup) or directly to the **Non-Hormonal Therapy pathway** (Topic 08) |
| **Conditions Often Mistaken for Absolute Contraindications (Safe with Transdermal / Specialist Care)** | • **Prior VTE or inherited thrombophilia** -> avoid oral E2; low-dose **transdermal E2 + micronized progesterone/Mirena** may be considered with hematology/specialist input<br>• **Prior MI (stabilized)** -> not an absolute contraindication; low-dose transdermal E2 + MP/dydrogesterone with cardiology input (see Topic 05)<br>• **Migraine WITH or WITHOUT aura** -> **NOT a contraindication** to physiological HRT; use **transdermal E2** (patches/gel) to avoid serum E2 fluctuations<br>• **Controlled hypertension, obesity (`BMI ≥30`), smoking, gallstones, hypertriglyceridemia, fibroids, treated endometriosis** -> prefer **transdermal E2**<br>• **`BRCA1/2` carrier without personal breast cancer** -> systemic MHT **recommended** post-RRSO until age `~52` | Pass Gate (once vitals/workup complete) with **Mandatory or Preferred Transdermal Route** flag |

## 4. Stage 1 Data Completeness Gate Logic

In the longitudinal clinical workflow synthesized from pre-HRT assessment, preventive screening, and cardiovascular risk stratification guidelines ([`04-hrt-starting-troubleshooting-stopping.md` §Pre-HRT Assessment](../markdown/red-whale/04-hrt-starting-troubleshooting-stopping.md#contraindications-to-hrt); [`menopause-intake-anamnesis-checklist.md`](../markdown/magid-course/menopause-intake-anamnesis-checklist.md); [`ima-preventive-medicine-guidelines-2022.md`](../markdown/magid-course/ima-preventive-medicine-guidelines-2022.md); [`mdcalc-10491-aha-prevent-calculator.md` §2](../markdown/magid-course/mdcalc-10491-aha-prevent-calculator.md#2-calculator-input-variables--validated-bounds)), Stage 1 evaluates whether it is clinically safe to formulate systemic therapy immediately or pause and issue a **Patient Workup Completion Sheet**:

* **Hard Stops (Must Resolve Before Prescribing Systemic MHT):**
  * Missing clinic **blood pressure** or **`BMI`** (required for VTE/stroke route stratification).
  * **Uninvestigated abnormal uterine / postmenopausal bleeding** or suspicious cervical/breast abnormality (`ABNORMAL_SUSPECTED` mammogram or abnormal Pap requiring colposcopy).
  * Uncontrolled severe hypertension (`≥180/110 mmHg`) or active liver disease awaiting LFT workup.
* **Age-Appropriate Screening & Targeted Lab Triggers (Order Workup, Do Not Unnecessarily Delay Safe Symptom Relief Unless Suspicious):**
  * Mammography check **only when age-eligible (`≥50y`, or `≥40y` if high family/clinical risk)** — *note: women `<50y` at average risk should NOT be blocked for lacking a screening mammogram!*
  * `CBC` + `ferritin` if heavy menstrual bleeding (`HMB`).
  * `FSH` + `E2` + `TSH` + `prolactin` + pregnancy exclusion if `<40y` (`POI` workup) or `40–44y` uncertain early menopause.
  * Fasting glucose/`HbA1c` + lipid panel + `eGFR` (creatinine) for `SCORE2`/`AHA PREVENT`/`ASCVD` calculation, `TSH` if on `levothyroxine` or symptomatic, `LFTs` if considering `fezolinetant`/`elinzanetant`, and Ashkenazi `BRCA` screening offer if untested.
