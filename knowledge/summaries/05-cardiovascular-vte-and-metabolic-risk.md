## 1. The Vascular Timing Hypothesis & 2025 FDA Label Update

* **Why Timing Relative to Menopause Matters:** In healthy early-postmenopausal endothelium (**age `<60y` or `<10 years` from FMP**), physiological 17β-estradiol stimulates endothelial nitric oxide (`NO`) synthase, inhibits vascular smooth-muscle Ca²⁺ channels, reduces LDL oxidation, and slows early atherogenesis. Conversely, when initiated **after age `60y` or `>10 years` post-FMP** in arteries harboring mature, lipid-rich atherosclerotic plaques, **oral estrogens** induce hepatic **matrix metalloproteinases (`MMPs`)** (destabilizing fibrous plaque caps) and procoagulant factors (`thrombus formation` upon plaque rupture) ([`bms-hrt-after-myocardial-infarction-2024.md` §Arterial Physiology](../markdown/magid-course/bms-hrt-after-myocardial-infarction-2024.md#what-does-hrt-do-to-normal-arteries); [`acog-cpg-13-vasomotor-sleep-mood-2026.md` §Hormone Therapy](../markdown/magid-course/acog-cpg-13-vasomotor-sleep-mood-2026.md#hormone-therapy)).
* **Clinical Trial Confirmation (`Cochrane` / `WHI` / `DOPS`):** Initiating MHT `<60y` or `<10y` from menopause reduces composite CHD mortality/nonfatal MI (`Cochrane RR 0.52, 95% CI 0.29–0.96`; Danish `DOPS` 16-yr RCT `HR 0.48`) without excess stroke when transdermal E2 is used, whereas initiating `>10y` post-menopause increases stroke (`RR 1.21`) and VTE (`RR 1.96`) ([`acog-cpg-13-vasomotor-sleep-mood-2026.md` §Hormone Therapy](../markdown/magid-course/acog-cpg-13-vasomotor-sleep-mood-2026.md#hormone-therapy); [`progestogen-selection-in-mht-srebnik.md` §Slide 21](../markdown/magid-course/progestogen-selection-in-mht-srebnik.md#slide-21-danish-rct-bmj-2012--16-year-outcomes-table-english)).
* **November 2025 FDA Boxed-Warning Removal:** Reflecting modern age-stratified and route-specific evidence, in 2025 the FDA removed the external class-wide boxed warnings for **cardiovascular disease, breast cancer, and probable dementia** from MHT product labels, adding guidance favoring initiation `<60y` or `<10y` from menopause onset (retaining only the endometrial cancer warning for unopposed estrogen) ([`acog-cpg-13-vasomotor-sleep-mood-2026.md` §Hormone Therapy](../markdown/magid-course/acog-cpg-13-vasomotor-sleep-mood-2026.md#hormone-therapy)).

* **`AHA PREVENT™` Calculator Inputs & Thresholds (`MDCalc #10491`, Ages `30–79` without known CVD):** Sex-specific, race-free CKM model using `Age`, `Total Cholesterol (130–320 mg/dL)`, `HDL-C (20–100 mg/dL)`, `SBP (90–200 mmHg)`, `Diabetes`, `Smoking`, `eGFR (15–140)`, `BP/Statin Rx`, and `BMI (18.5–39.9)` (plus optional `uACR`, `HbA1c`, `SDI`) to estimate **10-year** and **30-year** (ages `30–59`) Total CVD, ASCVD, and HF risk. Notably, **2025 AHA/ACC HTN** starts BP pharmacotherapy for Stage 1 HTN (`≥130/80 mmHg`) at **`10-yr Total CVD ≥7.5%`**, and **2026 ACC/AHA Dyslipidemia** considers statin therapy starting at **`≥3%` 10-yr PREVENT-ASCVD** (or `<3%` if `30-yr ASCVD ≥10%` or `LDL 160–189 mg/dL`) ([`mdcalc-10491-aha-prevent-calculator.md` §4](../markdown/magid-course/mdcalc-10491-aha-prevent-calculator.md#4-guideline-management-thresholds-linked-to-prevent)).

## 3. Venous Thromboembolism (`VTE`) & Stroke Risk Management

* **Oral vs. Transdermal Route:** Oral estrogen roughly **doubles baseline VTE risk** (`OR ~1.58–2.10`, peaking in the first 12 months of use) and increases ischemic stroke (`~30%` relative increase, predominantly `>60y`). **Transdermal 17β-estradiol (`patches`, `gels`, `spray`) shows NO statistically significant increase in VTE risk (`OR ~0.93–0.96`) — even at higher doses (`>50 mcg` patch) in UK `QResearch`/`CPRD` (`Vinogradova 2019`) and `ESTHER` studies** ([`08-hrt-and-risk-of-vte.md` §Evidence](../markdown/red-whale/08-hrt-and-risk-of-vte.md#main-recent-studies-looking-at-vte-risk-and-hrt); [`02-nice-guideline-menopause-2024.md`](../markdown/red-whale/02-nice-guideline-menopause-2024.md#discussing-risks-or-specific-health-outcomes-associated-with-hrt-use)).
* **Progestogen Effect on VTE:** Oral `MPA` (`OR ~2.1–2.7` combined with oral E) and **norpregnane** derivatives (`Canonico E3N HR 1.8`) amplify VTE risk, whereas **micronized progesterone**, **dydrogesterone**, and **`52 mg LNG-IUS`** do **not** increase VTE risk when paired with transdermal estradiol ([`08-hrt-and-risk-of-vte.md` §Progestogenic effect](../markdown/red-whale/08-hrt-and-risk-of-vte.md#progestogenic-effect); [`progestogen-selection-in-mht-srebnik.md` §Slide 23](../markdown/magid-course/progestogen-selection-in-mht-srebnik.md#slide-23-venous-thromboembolism-vte-risk-by-progestogen-class--e3n-study-canonico-et-al-2010)).
* **Recurrent VTE & Thrombophilia Testing Rules:**
  * **Do NOT perform routine thrombophilia screening** before starting HRT (even with family history of VTE — a negative panel does not exclude polygenic thrombosis risk, so management already mandates transdermal E2!) ([`08-hrt-and-risk-of-vte.md` §Thrombophilia testing](../markdown/red-whale/08-hrt-and-risk-of-vte.md#is-there-ever-a-role-for-thrombophilia-testing)).
  * In women with **personal history of VTE** or known high-risk thrombophilia (`Factor V Leiden`, prothrombin mutation, `Antithrombin`/`Protein C`/`S` deficiency), oral HRT is strictly contraindicated; **transdermal E2 + micronized progesterone/dydrogesterone/Mirena** did not increase recurrent VTE in French cohort studies (`Olie 2011`) and may be prescribed **in consultation with hematology / menopause specialist** (and is generally safe while therapeutically anticoagulated) ([`08-hrt-and-risk-of-vte.md` §Recurrent VTE & High-risk options](../markdown/red-whale/08-hrt-and-risk-of-vte.md#evidence-looking-at-the-risk-of-recurrent-vte-and-hrt-use)).

## 4. BMS 2024 Guidance: HRT After Myocardial Infarction (`Post-MI`)

Per the **British Menopause Society Tool for Clinicians (March 2024)** ([`bms-hrt-after-myocardial-infarction-2024.md`](../markdown/magid-course/bms-hrt-after-myocardial-infarction-2024.md)):

1. **Acute MI in a Woman Already Taking HRT:**
   * **Do NOT reflexively stop HRT permanently** at the time of acute MI (abrupt withdrawal can provoke severe vasomotor sympathetic surges; pause temporarily only if prolonged immobilization raises acute VTE risk).
   * Once stabilized, **switch any oral estrogen to low/ultra-low transdermal 17β-estradiol** (`25–50 mcg` patch or `0.5–1 mg` gel) and **switch any androgenic progestin (`MPA`, `NETA`) to micronized progesterone or dydrogesterone** (or `52 mg LNG-IUS`). Nationwide Finnish registry data (`>2,300` women) found no excess reinfarction or mortality with continued transdermal estradiol therapy ([`bms-hrt-after-myocardial-infarction-2024.md` §Acute MI on HRT](../markdown/magid-course/bms-hrt-after-myocardial-infarction-2024.md#what-happens-if-a-woman-on-hrt-sustains-an-mi)).
2. **Starting HRT De Novo in a Woman with Prior MI:**
   * Prior MI is **not an absolute contraindication** when severe VMS impair quality of life and non-hormonal options fail or are unsuitable.
   * Clarify **MI mechanism** with cardiology: standard plaque-rupture MI on optimal secondary prevention (statin, antiplatelet, BP control) vs. **`MINOCA` / coronary vasospasm / `SCAD` (spontaneous coronary artery dissection)**, where hormonal vascular effects are less predictable and require specialist cardiology co-management.
   * Initiate **lowest effective transdermal estradiol dose (`25 mcg` patch or `0.5 mg` gel)** + **micronized progesterone (`100–200 mg`) or dydrogesterone (`5–10 mg`)**; **local vaginal estrogen is always safe** ([`bms-hrt-after-myocardial-infarction-2024.md` §Previous MI needing HRT](../markdown/magid-course/bms-hrt-after-myocardial-infarction-2024.md#what-happens-if-a-woman-with-a-previous-mi-needs-hrt)).
