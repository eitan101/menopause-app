## 1. BMS 2024 / Dr. Merav Freiberg Algorithm for Unscheduled Bleeding on MHT

Unscheduled spotting/bleeding occurs in up to **40%** of women during the first months of `ccHRT`, but requires structured triage to avoid both over-investigation and missed endometrial pathology ([`11-problematic-bleeding-on-hrt.md`](../markdown/red-whale/11-problematic-bleeding-on-hrt.md); [`bleeding-and-side-effects-under-mht-freiberg.md` §Slides 9–24](../markdown/magid-course/bleeding-and-side-effects-under-mht-freiberg.md#slide-11-endometrial-hyperplasia--cancer-risk-factors--גורמי-סיכון-להיפרפלזיה-וסרטן-רחם-בנוטלות-hrt)).

### Step 1: Exclude Non-Endometrial / Adherence / Interaction Causes
Check adherence & timing of both E and P, patch detachment/skin absorption, missed pills/GI diarrhea, **new `GLP-1`/`GIP` therapy or enzyme inducers**, expired `Mirena` (`>5 years`!) or malpositioned `Mirena` strings, cervical polyps/ectropion (`speculum exam` + `Pap/hrHPV` status), STI (`chlamydia`), and pregnancy if perimenopausal ([`11-problematic-bleeding-on-hrt.md` §Causes](../markdown/red-whale/11-problematic-bleeding-on-hrt.md#causes-of-abnormal-bleeding-on-hrt); [`bleeding-and-side-effects-under-mht-freiberg.md` §Slide 10](../markdown/magid-course/bleeding-and-side-effects-under-mht-freiberg.md#slide-10-initial-assessment-of-unscheduled-bleeding--הערכת-אישה-המגיעה-עם-דימום-לא-צפוי-תחת-טיפול)).

### Step 2: Stratify Endometrial Hyperplasia / Cancer Risk (`BMS 2024`)

| Risk Category | Specific Factors (`BMS 2024` & `Freiberg Slide 11`) | Investigation Threshold |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **MAJOR Risk Factors** *(`≥1` triggers urgent investigation regardless of HRT duration)* | • **`BMI ≥ 40 kg/m²`**<br>• Hereditary predisposition (**`Lynch`** or **`Cowden`** syndrome)<br>• Prolonged **unopposed estrogen** (`>6 months`) or **under-opposed estrogen** (inadequate progestogen dose for moderate/high E2 tier, or `<12 d/mo` in `sHRT`)<br>• **Sequential HRT (`sHRT`) used `>5 years` in a woman started at age `≥45`**<br>• Prior endometrial hyperplasia / `Tamoxifen` use | **Urgent Suspicion-of-Cancer Pathway / Urgent `TVS` (`≤2 weeks`)** |
| **MINOR Risk Factors** *(`≥2` [BMS/Freiberg Slide 18] or `≥3` [Red Whale Hebrew summary] triggers `TVS`)* | • **`BMI 30–39.9 kg/m²`**<br>• **Diabetes mellitus** / insulin resistance<br>• **`PCOS`** / chronic anovulation / nulliparity / late menopause | **`≥2–3` Minor Factors ➔ Urgent `TVS` (`≤6 weeks`)** even within first 6 months |

*References:* [`11-problematic-bleeding-on-hrt.md` §Risk stratification](../markdown/red-whale/11-problematic-bleeding-on-hrt.md#risk-stratification-for-endometrial-cancer), [`bleeding-and-side-effects-under-mht-freiberg.md` §Slides 11 & 18](../markdown/magid-course/bleeding-and-side-effects-under-mht-freiberg.md#slide-18-immediate-cancer-pathway-red-flags--הפניה-מיידית-לשלילת-סרטן), [`hebrew-summary-hrt-and-progesterone.md` §Endometrial Cancer Risk Factors](../markdown/red-whale/hebrew-summary-hrt-and-progesterone.md#endometrial-cancer-risk-factors--גורמי-סיכון-לסרטן-רירית-הרחם).

### Step 3: Time-Window Triage & `TVS` Endometrial Thickness (`ET`) Cutoffs

1. **Low-Risk Woman Within `6 Months` of Starting HRT or `3 Months` of Dose/Regimen Change** *(light/spotting, normal cervix)*:
   * **No `TVS` needed initially** — reassure and **optimize HRT regimen** over up to 6 months ([`bleeding-and-side-effects-under-mht-freiberg.md` §Slide 14](../markdown/magid-course/bleeding-and-side-effects-under-mht-freiberg.md#slide-14-grace-period-for-regimen-adjustment-in-low-risk-women--מתי-לבצע-בירור-התאמת-טיפול-בסיכון-נמוך)).
2. **Urgent `TVS` Within `6 Weeks` (or Urgent 2-Week Cancer Referral if Red Flags):**
   * Bleeding persisting **`>6 months`** after starting HRT or **`>3 months`** after regimen adjustment; new bleeding starting *after* `≥6m` of established amenorrhea on `ccHRT`; **heavy/prolonged/worsening** bleeding; suspicious cervix; or Major / multiple Minor risk factors ([`11-problematic-bleeding-on-hrt.md` §Assessment](../markdown/red-whale/11-problematic-bleeding-on-hrt.md#assessment-and-investigation-of-unscheduled-bleeding-on-hrt); [`bleeding-and-side-effects-under-mht-freiberg.md` §Slides 15–18](../markdown/magid-course/bleeding-and-side-effects-under-mht-freiberg.md#slide-17-visual-flowchart--נשים-הבוחרות-להפסיק-ה-hrt-והתוויות-ל-us-דחוף)).
   * *If the patient chooses to **stop HRT**:* if bleeding completely ceases within **`4 weeks`** (and no major risk factors), no `TVS` is needed; if bleeding persists at `4 weeks` off HRT ➔ **urgent `2-week` postmenopausal bleeding cancer workup** ([`bleeding-and-side-effects-under-mht-freiberg.md` §Slide 17](../markdown/magid-course/bleeding-and-side-effects-under-mht-freiberg.md#slide-17-visual-flowchart--נשים-הבוחרות-להפסיק-ה-hrt-והתוויות-ל-us-דחוף)).
3. **Interpreting `TVS` Endometrial Thickness (`ET`) on HRT:**
   * **On Continuous Combined (`ccHRT`):** Reassuring if **`ET ≤ 4 mm`**; **`ET > 4 mm`** (or focal polyp/incomplete view) ➔ **endometrial biopsy (`Pipelle`) / diagnostic hysteroscopy** ([`bleeding-and-side-effects-under-mht-freiberg.md` §Slides 19–20](../markdown/magid-course/bleeding-and-side-effects-under-mht-freiberg.md#slide-20-endometrial-thickness-cutoffs-for-urgent-biopsy--hysteroscopy--רירית-מעובה-ביופסיה-והיסטרוסקופיה-דחופה)).
   * **On Sequential Combined (`sHRT`):** Reassuring if **`ET ≤ 7 mm`**; **`ET > 7 mm`** ➔ **endometrial biopsy / hysteroscopy**.
   * **Incidental `ET` in a Completely ASYMPTOMATIC Woman on HRT:** Do **not** routinely investigate `ET ≤ 10 mm` in the absence of bleeding ([`11-problematic-bleeding-on-hrt.md` §Asymptomatic thickened endometrium](../markdown/red-whale/11-problematic-bleeding-on-hrt.md#what-do-i-do-about-a-thickened-endometrium-reported-in-an-asymptomatic-woman-on-hrt)).
4. **Practical Regimen Adjustments to Stop Benign Unscheduled Bleeding (Once Pathology Excluded):**
   * Verify adherence; lower excessive E2 dose if VMS allow; **increase progestogen dose/duration** (e.g. `Utrogestan 100 → 200 mg` continuous, or `14–21 d/mo` in `sHRT`); switch oral MP to **synthetic progestin (`Duphaston`, `Activelle`, `Evorel Conti`)** for tighter endometrial stability; or insert **`52 mg Mirena` (Gold Standard)** ([`11-problematic-bleeding-on-hrt.md` §HRT optimisation](../markdown/red-whale/11-problematic-bleeding-on-hrt.md#management-of-bleeding-issues-on-hrt-hrt-optimisation); [`bleeding-and-side-effects-under-mht-freiberg.md` §Slides 22–23](../markdown/magid-course/bleeding-and-side-effects-under-mht-freiberg.md#slide-22-optimizing-regimen--role-of-mirena--התאמת-הטיפול-להפחתת-דימום-לא-צפוי)).

## 2. Side-Effect Troubleshooting Matrix (Estrogenic vs. Progestogenic)

Most adaptation side effects settle within **`3 months`** ([`04-hrt-starting-troubleshooting-stopping.md` §Troubleshooting](../markdown/red-whale/04-hrt-starting-troubleshooting-stopping.md#troubleshooting-and-managing-side-effects); [`bleeding-and-side-effects-under-mht-freiberg.md` §Slides 4–7 & 25–27](../markdown/magid-course/bleeding-and-side-effects-under-mht-freiberg.md#slide-25-managing-breast-tenderness--mastalgia--גודש-כאב-שדיים)):

| SymptomCluster | Primary Culprit | Targeted Adjustments |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Breast tenderness / mastalgia, nausea, leg cramps, headaches** | **Estrogen excess / rapid E2 swings** *(or synthetic progestin)* | Reassure for first 8–12w; **reduce estrogen dose**; switch oral → **transdermal E2** (steadier levels, less nausea/headache); switch synthetic progestin → `Utrogestan`, **`Duavive` (`TSEC`)**, or **`Livial` (`Tibolone`)** |
| **Daytime drowsiness, dizziness, sedation** | **Oral Micronized Progesterone (`Utrogestan`)** *(neurosteroid `allopregnanolone`)* | Take strictly **at bedtime** (avoid taking with high-fat evening meal, which spikes absorption and sedation!); or switch to non-sedating **`Duphaston` (`dydrogesterone`)** or `Mirena` |
| **PMS-like irritability, low mood, acne, seborrhea** | **Androgenic C19 Progestins (`NETA`: `Activelle`, `Evorel Conti`)** | Switch to non-androgenic **`Utrogestan`**, **`Duphaston` / `Femoston`**, **`Angeliq` (`drospirenone`)**, or **`Duavive`** |
| **Fluid retention / bloating** | Estrogen or mineralocorticoid effect | Switch to transdermal E2; consider anti-mineralocorticoid **`Angeliq` (`drospirenone`)** or `Utrogestan` |
| **Local patch skin rash / poor adhesion** | Patch adhesive matrix | Apply to clean hairless buttock/hip (avoid oils/lotions; air patch briefly after opening; trial topical fluticasone spray under site in severe cases) or switch patch → **estradiol gel (`Estrogel` / `Sandrena`)** |

## 3. Critical Drug Interactions with MHT (`BMS August 2025` & `Red Whale #14`)

| Co-Prescribed Medication | Clinical Interaction Mechanism | Mandatory Management Rule |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`GLP-1` / `GIP` Incretin Mimetics**<br>*(`Tirzepatide` / `Mounjaro`, `Semaglutide` / `Ozempic` / `Wegovy`, `Liraglutide`, `Dulaglutide`)* | Delayed gastric emptying reduces peak/total absorption of **oral progestogens**, risking **endometrial hyperplasia** and unscheduled bleeding! | **BMS 2025:**<br>1. **First-line:** **Transdermal E2 + non-oral progestogen (`52mg Mirena` preferred, or combined patch `Evorel Conti`)**.<br>2. **If continuing oral progestogen:** **Increase oral progestogen dose (e.g. double / high-tier dose) for `4 weeks` upon starting the `GLP-1`/`GIP` AND for `4 weeks` after EVERY `GLP-1` dose escalation!** Investigate any persistent unscheduled bleeding promptly |
| **`Lamotrigine`** *(epilepsy / bipolar)* | **Oral estrogen** induces hepatic `UGT1A4` glucuronidation, **dropping serum lamotrigine levels by up to 50%** (breakthrough seizures!) — and levels spike to toxicity during hormone-free intervals! | **Strongly prefer transdermal E2** + check baseline/follow-up serum lamotrigine levels jointly with neurology |
| **Hepatic Enzyme Inducers**<br>*(`Carbamazepine`, `Phenytoin`, `Phenobarbital`, `Rifampicin`, `St John's Wort`, certain ARVs)* | `CYP3A4` induction accelerates clearance of **both oral AND transdermal estrogens and oral progestogens**, causing VMS relapse and breakthrough bleeding / endometrial under-protection | Stop `St John's Wort`; prefer **transdermal E2** (titrated clinically) + **`52mg Mirena`** (local uterine action unaffected by hepatic CYP3A4!) |
| **`Levothyroxine` (`Eltroxin` / `Euthyrox`)** | **Oral estrogen** increases hepatic `TBG`, binding free `T4` and raising `TSH` | Prefer **transdermal E2** (minimal `TBG` effect); if starting/stopping *oral* estrogen, **recheck `TSH` in `6–12 weeks`** |

*References:* [`14-drug-interactions-with-hrt.md`](../markdown/red-whale/14-drug-interactions-with-hrt.md#bms-guidance-on-hrt-in-women-on-incretin-based-therapies), [`menopause-mht-treatment-renana-teomim.md` §Slide 5](../markdown/magid-course/menopause-mht-treatment-renana-teomim.md#slide-5-indications-for-non-oral-estrogen--למי-נעדיף-טיפול-e-שאינו-פומי).

## 4. Monitoring Schedule, Duration & Stopping MHT (`Tapering` vs. `Abrupt`)

* **Follow-Up Schedule:** Initial review at **`3 months`** after starting or changing MHT (assess symptom control, side effects, bleeding, adherence, `BP`), then **at least annually** (`BP`, `BMI`, risk-benefit review, breast/cervical/bone screening status, `Mirena` 5-yr expiry check) ([`04-hrt-starting-troubleshooting-stopping.md` §Monitoring HRT](../markdown/red-whale/04-hrt-starting-troubleshooting-stopping.md#monitoring-hrt); [`acog-cpg-13-vasomotor-sleep-mood-2026.md` §Hormone Therapy](../markdown/magid-course/acog-cpg-13-vasomotor-sleep-mood-2026.md#hormone-therapy)).
* **No Arbitrary Age Cutoff (`NICE` / `BMS` / `ACOG 2026`):** Do **not** force discontinuation at age 60 or 65; continue as long as individualized benefits outweigh risks (consider stepping down estrogen dose and switching oral → transdermal with advancing age) ([`04-hrt-starting-troubleshooting-stopping.md` §Duration](../markdown/red-whale/04-hrt-starting-troubleshooting-stopping.md#duration-of-use-and-stopping-hrt); [`acog-cpg-13-vasomotor-sleep-mood-2026.md` §Hormone Therapy](../markdown/magid-course/acog-cpg-13-vasomotor-sleep-mood-2026.md#hormone-therapy)).
* **Stopping MHT:** Long-term VMS recurrence rates (~40%–50%) are **similar at 1 year whether stopped gradually or abruptly**, though **gradual tapering over `3–6 months`** (stepping down E2 dose while maintaining progestogen coverage until E2 stops, and continuing local vaginal estrogen if needed for GSM!) reduces short-term rebound VMS ([`04-hrt-starting-troubleshooting-stopping.md` §Tapering vs abrupt](../markdown/red-whale/04-hrt-starting-troubleshooting-stopping.md#tapering-hrt-vs-abrupt-withdrawal)).
